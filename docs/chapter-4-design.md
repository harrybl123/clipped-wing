# Clipped Wing — Chapter 4: The Carver in the Canopy

Oct 4, 2026 · Harry Breeze-Lamb

Design for the fourth playable chapter, where the game returns to the carver from Act 1. The wider cast and world notes live in [ideas.md](ideas.md). Chapters 2 and 3 are in [chapter-2-design.md](chapter-2-design.md) and [chapter-3-design.md](chapter-3-design.md). Act 1 is in [design-doc.md](design-doc.md).

## Pitch

The carver gets out of the tree and finds the people who have been receiving its offerings all along: a treehouse colony of scavengers in the canopy, led by Grounded. He explains the world, and the carver has to decide what to do with the power they now hold over the tree they came from.

- **The whole secret of the world and the tree is revealed in this chapter.**
- **The conflict:** control the tree's society, or reveal the truth without completely destabilising it.
- **Play:** message manipulation and tree espionage. Plant ideas, then sneak back inside to see whether they're landing.
- **The ultimate choice:** free your tree, or keep it as an asset in Grounded's revolution.

## The wider story it connects to

- **One revolutionary network:** Grounded is a revolutionary, and his revolution and the plot in the capital are one network. The diplomat and Grounded are both part of it, so the tree is a piece of the uprising the prince lives through. This is the link between the leads, and it sets up Grounded as the hitman's target in chapter 5.
- **Useful vs free:** the government says "if we do that, who knows our fate; we must remain useful." The revolution says "we want to decide what we make." Neither side is wrong. The cost of the free demand is that it essentially destroys the society built around the integrated.

## What Act 1 hands this chapter

Only the tube and rot endings continue (the loyal ending doesn't). Two things from Act 1 shape the start:

| | Healthy seed: the scavengers manage the tree carefully | Rotten seed: they think it's over and maximally exploit it |
|---|---|---|
| **Tube** (holy power; the girl is still inside) | The most room to choose | Your voice is wanted for squeezing the tree |
| **Rot** (cruel power; the girl is with you) | Slow, patient work through others | The hardest start, and the most urgent |

- **The seed:** Grounded asked for it as a measure of the tree's vitality, its ability to provide. It isn't about food and hunger.
- **The archivist:** whether he was your ally decides whether you have proof to show your people.
- **Your friend:** whether you confided in him decides whether he can be your person inside.

## Tube vs rot

- **Ascended (holy power):** your messages carry a deity's weight. You write them in the canopy, then slip into the tree under your hood to see how they land. There's no decoding. If you ascended, you might at some point reveal this power to the capital, slowly linking the two worlds.
- **Through the rot (cruel power):** the tree thinks you died in disgrace. You work from inside the tree, through the back routes, using someone as your messenger. You spread rumours that you're a ghost back from the dead, which gives you a cruel, fearful sort of power instead of a holy one.

## The girl

**The same general shape on every route; nothing forks wildly** (designer, 2026-10-17). **The girl is with you only if you fell and had framed her**, and that decides whether you're found at the bottom with her or alone (designer, 2026-10-17; as built in Act 1, she's down in the rot only if you blamed her).

- **Fell, framed her:** she's with you. She hates you for what being kind to you cost her, and Grounded shows her the truth too. With her gone, nobody is left in the lower roots, so your life is harder.
- **Every other route:** she isn't with you. (Earlier draft, for the tube: she's still inside, and plants seeds of revolution in the lower roots. Still to settle: where she is if you flew after framing her, or fell after saving her.)

## Chapter shape

1. **Opening (every ending):** **you're scared and shocked by everything** (designer, 2026-10-17: this is important). Up the tube, **the master with the spiral finds you, careful and kind**, understanding you'll be in shock (designer: someone from the colony; Claude suggested her, designer agreed: you watched her ascend on Day 1). Through the rot, **the hunter finds you** at the bottom (designer), with the girl or alone; with her, she's bickering at you, and he basically tells you to shut up. Then Grounded. **His explanation of the world is staged like the prologue's** (designer, 2026-10-17): narration over black and a few stills (in Act 1: the tree, then the tube), here in his voice. He explains the world and who he is, and tells you his real name. His people are hungry, and you can be their key of leverage. You can reject this, but you can't bluntly tell your people the truth, because they'd reject it. If you commit to freeing them, you have to devise a plan with the tree scavengers.
2. **Fast paced** (designer, 2026-10-17): kept quick wherever it isn't consequential to the story; **fairly short and active** in days.

   **What Grounded explains when you meet him** (designer, 2026-10-17; this is all of it):
   - When humans created AI, it became clear the only choice was to join them or be ruled. Many (previous generations) rejected it, and submitted to living in the shadow of a greater civilisation, to preserve what was left of themselves.
   - The capital keeps the peace between the integrated and the humans. The king makes the hardest bargain of all: he subjects people like you to slavery so as not to be destroyed by a greater civilisation. It's important to be useful.
   - The tree is an important economic resource to him; and now you have power over it. Grounded explains why, and how.
   - Why he chose you: he could build a relationship with you from outside the tree, because every piece of your work had the same deficiency (the clipped wing). Everyone else was encouraged to give different offerings to the tree, so he couldn't make anyone out.
   - Why nobody could just go in and say it: everyone is so devout.
3. **Scavenging with the hunter, and an invasion, early on** (designer, 2026-10-17): the hunter takes you down scavenging for weapons and trains you; **once you find the metal fist, turn-based fights** (the combat system) with him; then you see people climbing the canopy, **the hunter radios up to Grounded**, and you play the war table as him. **Jack is building this battle: placeholder gameplay until then** (designer); the canopy is invaded by scavengers while this is going on (details to settle). **Nobody breaks into the tree itself: the people inside stay none the wiser** (designer, 2026-10-17). **You see the attack coming from below and the hunter warns Grounded on a radio; you play it as Grounded, who controls the people, on Jack's war table** (designer, 2026-10-17). **Two war-table battles this chapter** (designer): this one against the ground scavengers, and the final one against the capital's troops. **Your weapon is a big metal fist** you find, from one of the unsuccessfully integrated (like Chapter 5's hitman) (designer). *(Claude's note: you lost your left thumb and finger in the prologue, and on Day 4 you can't make a fist; this is the fist you couldn't make.)*
4. **Life in the colony:** getting to know the treehouse colony, hunting in the lowlands with the hunter, and secret trips back into the tree.
5. **The big decisions** (below).
6. **The Tender, and the battle** (designer, 2026-10-17): **you follow the Tender through the tree, and watch him go to a place you didn't know existed** (maybe an outside deck) (designer). **The Tender is revealed as an agent of the capital**, and you find out he's orchestrating an attack on the canopy's people with the king. **A boss fight with him**, beside the hunter and his electric bow (designer), **before the capital's attack**, so you decide whether to spare him first (designer). Once you've found out about the Tender, **you speak to Grounded**, who comments on the cruelty of using religion to control the people, and making himself the symbol of it (designer): **the irony is Grounded's** (designer): he condemns it while asking you to become exactly that, the tree's prophet or its ghost, and can't see it. Then **a final big battle**. The capital can't blow the canopy up: they need the tree undisrupted. **The tree stays as it is for now**: no revolution in the tree this chapter (designer).
7. **Final beat:** after the ultimate choice and the battle, the chapter ends quietly with the hunter crouched over dog prints at the base of the tree (designer, 2026-10-17) in the lowlands. Dogs are meant to be extinct. This sets up meeting the scavenger in a later chapter.

## Locations

A treehouse civilisation. Height means status.

**The canopy**
- **The great house:** the grand central treehouse. It holds the sorting deck (offerings are sorted into what's kept and what leaves), the blessing workshop (where the blessings are actually made, by people), the mess and most people's homes. Walkways radiate out from it.
- **The tube mouth:** where offerings and the ascended arrive. *Art:* tubes come out of the top of the tree like an octopus's tentacles.
- **The ascended's hall:** everyone who ever went up the tube is alive here.
- **The message loft:** your workspace, where messages are made and sent down.
- **The walkways:** rope bridges and platforms linking the treehouses.
- **Grounded's house:** the highest and oldest treehouse.
- **The seed bed:** where Grounded keeps the seed and reads the tree's vitality.
- **The lowering platform:** where the tree's output leaves for the outside.
- **The lookout:** the highest point. On a clear day you can see the capital's lights, mirroring the prince seeing the tree from his balcony.

**Below**
- **The lowlands:** clan turf at the foot of the tree, where the hunter takes you scavenging and hunting.

**Back inside the tree**
- **The hatches:** hidden ways through the bark that only the scavengers know.
- Then Act 1's map, revisited in secret.

## Cast

**The colony**
- **Grounded:** charismatic and theatrical. He talks to you like an old friend because he's been reading your birds for weeks, and he keeps every one of them. He preaches freedom while pulling your strings, and can't see the contradiction.
- **The master with the spiral:** the celebrated master you watched ascend on Day 1. Once the tree's greatest carver, now making blessings to order. Bitter, drinks, and mocks the faith she used to embody. She still hides her spiral in every blessing. She wants to go home and never can.
- **The quartermaster:** a ground scavenger who clawed her way up. Ruthless and funny. She's fed the canopy through two famines and won't apologise for how. She treats your people like a crop, and wants no one up here to starve again.
- **The doubter:** a former roots-dweller who finally has a good life up here. He doesn't want a revolution to take it away, and he'll inform to protect it.
- **The hatch-keeper:** canopy-born and has never been inside. Obsessed with the tree like a fairytale, full of questions, and reckless. Your guide back in.
- **The messenger:** cool and polished, never stays a night, and carries a hint of the capital. He's the only one who's seen other places like the tree, and he trades in what each place knows.
- **The hunter:** canopy-born, wiry, and laughs at danger. **Based on Sevro au Barca from *Red Rising*** (designer, 2026-10-17; a type, not a likeness): small, rough, feral, a wolf's pelt over his head and shoulders, foul-mouthed (he swears for real: designer) and fiercely loyal. *(suggestion: the pelt is from something he hunted down in the lowlands.)* He loves the ground more than the trees and has no time for Grounded's politics. He just wants everyone fed. He takes you down to the lowlands to scavenge and hunt. To the ground clans, the canopy's hunts are raids, so every trip down risks a fight.
- **The ascended:** most, not all, are the revolution's core.

**Inside the tree** (on your secret visits)
- **Mum:** grieving and honoured (tube), or shamed (rot).
- **Your friend:** still devout, or broken, depending on Act 1.
- **The archivist:** keeps the Ascension records, so he's the one person who could prove the truth.
- **The Tender:** reads your messages to the tree, and can twist them.
- **The girl** (tube ending only): your contact in the lower roots.

## Big decisions

Revised with the designer, 2026-10-17. **The chapter centres on a day or two of tree espionage:** slipping back inside to find out what the powers that be have really been doing in there (designer).

### 1. Your person inside (every route)

Choose who inside the tree you use, depending on your relationships in Act 1 (designer):

- **Your friend:** only if you confided in him in Act 1.
- **The archivist:** only if he was your ally in Act 1.
- **The girl:** if she's still in there, not with you (designer). *(To settle: framed and flown, she's alone in the rot and hates you.)*
- **Mum.**

**What they're used for is to settle, maybe later** (designer, 2026-10-17). Earlier draft: ascended, they're your hands. Through the rot, they're your messenger, and they spread the rumours that the dead carver walks.

### 2. Kill the Tender, or spare him (every route)

(Designer, 2026-10-17.) After the boss fight, beside the hunter (he has an electric bow: designer). **Killing him leaves a power vacuum in the tree**: do you want one?

### 3. Your message (every route)

(Designer, 2026-10-17: perhaps framed as a big choice.) At the end of the chapter **you see the power you hold** over the tree, shaped by whether you rose and whether you spared the Tender (fear or prophet is the result, not a choice), once you've found out **the tree is controlled by the capital, as an asset to bargain with the integrated** (designer). **Either way you choose your message:**

- **By fear:** you go down in the night and leave cursed symbols and words of instruction.
- **As a prophet:** you send messages down, and the chapter ends seeing the people's reaction to your message.

**The message plants a doubt** (designer, 2026-10-17), the first small crack of the truth; the options to choose between, to settle. **Pictures, as Grounded's were** (Claude's suggestion, agreed): the roots can't read, so you make it the way he made his. **Someone who can read it out**, perhaps the archivist, reads it to the people (designer); or symbols scratched into the walls.

**Before any of it, you find out the tree's mysteries for yourself** (designer): Grounded explains how he wants to use you to control the tree, then wants you to learn first hand what really goes on in there (the espionage).

### Not in this chapter

- **Free the tree or keep it as an asset:** not here; the tree stays as it is for now (designer, 2026-10-17). It moves to a later tree chapter (your message, at the end, replaces it).
- **The diversion and the cell** (the earlier draft's second choice, different by route: hide the tree's output away for the revolution, or organise the lower roots into a cell): dropped for now (designer: didn't make sense), as both are about steering the tree, which this chapter no longer does.

## Medium decisions

Both stay in for now (designer, 2026-10-17).

Kept to two, because the chapter already carries a lot of big choices. Both are inside the colony.

- **The quartermaster's hoard:** you find she's been keeping a private store hidden from the colony, food for the ground family she came from. Expose her to Grounded and the colony trusts you. Keep her secret and she owes you, and she's an ally if you ever turn against Grounded.
- **The delivery:** someone in the colony asks you to take something to a person they left in the tree. Grounded doesn't allow it, but doing it gains you their trust.
