## Beats (suggestion)

### 1. Morning: hungry, and your reply
- No blessings for anyone in the roots: the elders have cut everyone's share. Mum puts her bowl in
  front of you and says she isn't hungry.
- The reply: the plate (many hands pointing at you, a hand reaching down: "they blame me; help me"),
  the seed sealed inside the hollow bird.

### 2. The shrine at dawn: something's wrong
- He doesn't weigh it. He drops it into a separate basket, marked. Voices behind the curtain (the
  preacher's among them). "Noon. Where we said." "Go home. Stay home today."

### 3. The market: blamed
- The stalls are nearly bare; a woman has fainted in the queue. You try to buy food with a carving.
- They see who it is. "It's the carver. It's its fault." "Since it started, the baskets come down
  half empty." "My children haven't eaten in two days because of you." Something's thrown; a man
  grabs at you; you run. (Suggestion: a short chase out of the market, or just a scripted scene.)
- You can't go anywhere looking like yourself any more.

### 4. The lower roots: a disguise
- If you've met the boy: "Back again? Business is booming." If not, the girl takes you to him.
- A hooded cloak (patched, lower-roots cloth) for food. With it on, people don't know you.

### 5. The tail
- The Tender and the preacher leave the shrine and cross the market, then go down into the back
  routes. You follow: stay within range, keep out of sight. At stop points he looks back, or stops
  to talk; get too close or be seen and you're back at the start of the tail; fall too far behind
  and you lose him.
- The meeting, through a grate: the food really is running out; the blessings coming down are
  fewer every week; the roots will turn on the elders unless someone's blamed. "Give them the
  carver." Tonight's hearing is a formality. And the lower-roots girl seen with you: "Bring her in
  too."

### 6. The evening: the bitten bird
- Your bird comes back with teeth marks. The crowd: screams, prayers, someone faints, the preacher
  calls it rot made flesh, people back away from you as if you'd done it.
- You: something out there bit it. Hungry? Angry? A warning? You don't know.

### 7. Night: message 5, the knock, the interrogation
- Decode message 5 at home. Then guards bang on the door: 15 seconds to hide the plate. (Kept simple:
  if it's still out, they take it, and it's held against you.)
- The shrine: the stool under the lamp; the Tender, the preacher, the elders. They go through what's
  different about you: the fledgling, the weight, the archive, the lower roots at night, the teeth.
- They bring in the girl. You save her (a madman: custody) or blame her (the lower rot: home under
  guard).
