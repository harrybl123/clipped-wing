# Sequence plan: Chapter 2, Part 3: The squeeze (Days 7–9)

Planned 2026-10-08; Day 7 built (`story/borderland/chapter2-day7.ink`; dev `?chapter=2&day=7`), see "Day 7 as built" below. **(doc)** = [the design doc](../../chapter-2-design.md); **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal. Part of [the chapter's structure](../../chapter-2-story.md). Text here is a sketch of the beats, not lines.

## Decided
- **The structure's row stands** (designer: "about right"): raids land wherever you've been; suspicion spreads through the bar, den and brothel; red herrings; the scout. Medium choices: your friend's secret, the gambler's debt, the rival drop. Big choice 2: the rival line.
- **It ramps:** drama and consequences. **(designer)**
- **We feel the earlier choices** (designer): your brother (big 1), the dog, the spar's wager and what you told the bar, the gambler, the board.
- **Pull every thread from Part 2** (designer): the board, the gambler, the strap with the circle's mark, the madam's girl who vanished, the son, the enforcer, the watcher, the fence, your brother. **Not the relationship scores** (designer): they stay quiet counters for the endings, never shown or paid off here.

## The shape (suggestion)
Three days, each tighter than the last. Day 7 is the town turning on you. Day 8 is you digging for the truth while they dig into you. Day 9 is the night watch and the scout. Each day has one fight and one medium choice; big choice 2 closes the part.

| Day | Turn of the screw | Medium choice | Fight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | The town turns: a raid where you'd been, a mark on your door, the enforcer's accusation at the tally | the gambler's debt | the debt collectors at the den |
| 8 | You find the drop; the son tails you; the enforcer searches your shack; your friend's lie | the rival drop; your friend's secret | the drop's guards, with the dog |
| 9 | A night watch on the line; the scout; your brother's last beat | — | the scout, after a chase over the scrap stacks |

Big choice 2 (the rival line) is the last thing in Part 3. Part 4 (the reveal) follows straight on, no gap.

## Consequences, thread by thread (suggestion)
- **Your brother (big 1):** at the compound he parrots the boss, then tells him about the dog (Day 9: the frame closes, as in the doc); at home, the chalk on your door, the shack search he stalls, and his nights out with the kid (he's the witness in Part 4).
- **The dog:** the frame's evidence (its bed in the shack search), and the way to the drop. It's under the floor through the search, and the boss learns of it on one path.
- **What you told the bar** (`told_where`): a picker lost in the sunk town, or the son finding rival tracks in the deep ground; a won spar means the son's own story.
- **The son:** he tails you at dawn (Day 8).
- **The gambler:** comes due on Day 7; helped twice, he arms you; refused, he's at the enforcer's table.
- **The board:** the raids follow it; you can lie to it on Day 7 and see the next raid land on the lie.
- **The strap with the circle's mark:** the queue looks at it on Day 7; the scout reads it on Day 9.
- **The madam's girl:** her bead at the drop; the madam tells you how she went (marked a rat, out past the tree's edge: where you'll go).
- **The fence:** a red herring on Day 9.
- **The enforcer:** loudest accuser (Day 7), searches your shack (Day 8): the doc's red herring.
- **The watcher:** gives the watch; takes your friend in, or the scout, if you hand them over.
- **The old scavenger:** hurt in a raid; the one who doesn't believe it's you.

## Fighters (suggestion, unique abilities, nothing reused)
- **The debt collectors** (the den): **Shakedown** (takes an item, or SP, if it lands), **Pin** (one holds you while the other hits: your next reaction is harder), a heavy **Cosh**.
- **The drop's guards** (the deep ground): rival clan, long staves; **Sweep** (low, hits you and the dog), **Ward** (one shields the other for a round).
- **The scout:** fast; **Slip** (dodges your next blow outright unless the dog Sniffs first), **Dust** (blinds, like the stragglers' Grit but on the dog: it can't Sniff for a round), a quick **Cut**.
- **Her new move, if you helped the gambler:** his weapon (to design: a weighted chain or loaded bones, **(suggestion)**).

## Day 7 as built (designer: build day by day, a rough structure first, then playtest; more fights, and fights with the dog)
**Rebuilt after the first playtest** (designer: the story was unclear; it snapped from good to bad with no build-up; set up the raids, and that people think you're an informant). The turn now happens in steps, each one on screen, and the reason it points at you is stated: **every night you put a false patch on the board to keep the dog secret, and the raiders keep hitting the patch the board says you'll be on.** You're never there, and your alibi is the dog.
- **End of Day 6, the night of the first raid** (new): the tally house is full of it. The boss: the other clan crossed the line in daylight, the first time since the truce; they knew where our people would be; "Somebody in this room is talking to the other side. An informant." The enforcer looks at you; the boss covers for you ("She's the last person who'd have told them"); the watcher looks at his chalk. In bed: "Somebody who reads the board. And you're on the board." Card: **Two weeks on** (was "A month on").
1. **Dawn at the hollow:** the false patches, the mast raided last week (your patch), "informant" said in the bar; last night you put down the old wells. **The sunk town with the dog:** the tickers (`tickers`; Latch), the lost picker's lamp if you told the bar the sunk town.
2. **The old wells** (new): shouting as you send the dog home; the wells raided at first light, people hurt, sacks gone. A picker: "She was down for the wells. So where was she?" You can't say. The old scavenger puts it together: the far end, the mast, the wells, "every time where the board says you'll be, and you're never there. That's what an informant looks like."
3. **The yard:** the word got there first; what you told the bar after the spar (the lost picker's sister and his lamp, the son's tracks, or the son's version).
4. **Your brother at the compound** (if he moved): "even family".
5. **The den** (medium choice): the gambler's debt; the house's men (`debt_collectors`; Shakedown winds you); his chain if you help (`has_chain`; **its move isn't in the fights yet**).
6. **Tribute:** the enforcer lists the three raids, "every one where she said she'd be", and "four times the tribute; unless somebody's paying them"; the boss: "She's one of mine." The board: which lie tonight.
7. **The yard at night:** the traders pack up; pickers waiting (`angry_pickers`; Pile in). Lose, and they cut your strap.
8. **Your door** (moved to last): the rat chalked while you were at the tally; your brother out scrubbing it, crying ("The kid says everyone's saying you're telling the other clan things"), set on finding the real one; or you alone in the dark. Then bed. Day 8 isn't built.
- **After the second playtest** (designer): the gambler finds you in the yard and asks you to come and stand there ("nobody touches anybody with the boss's favourite in the room"), so the den has a reason; what you did at the den goes round, and the enforcer uses it at the tally (fought: "put two of the house's men on the floor, for a debtor", and the boss warns you off the house's business; paid: "where's a picker get a week to throw away?"; walked away: "left him to them"); **what they think you're doing is said plainly** (the old scavenger at the wells, the enforcer at the tally): you sell the board, telling the other clan which squares we'll dig, keep yourself clear, and they pay you in finds, which is where your four-times hauls came from; **the board is a grid** of the fields, rows one to twelve with the landmarks, and you name a square (the mast, row six; the old wells, row eleven; tonight the hull, row two, or the ditch, row nine: `board_mark`).
