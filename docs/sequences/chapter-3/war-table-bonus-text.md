# War table: what the bonuses say (for Jack to rewrite)

Every piece of text a bonus shows in the skirmish, as it is now. "Hover" means the card you see with the mouse on
the place; "Banner" is the big word across the screen. Numbers are the skirmish's. Rewrite anything; the code
fills in the numbers.

## On the map (hover, before anyone takes it)

Each ends with "(N turns left)".

- **Rumble:** "A rumble: whoever gets here first sets it off. Everyone next to it takes 1 to 3."
  - Banner when set off: "Rumble!" / "Every squad round it is hit, yours or theirs. The squad that set it off is spared."
- **Elite crest:** "A crest: whoever gets here first can put in one of their elite squad."
- **Mystery (?):** "A mystery: first here finds out. A diviner, a thief, the trickster, or the plague."
- **The Diviner (eye):** "The diviner: first here draws one of three cards."
- **The Trickster (crossed arrows):** "The trickster: first here changes every street on the table."
- **Audience:** "An audience: first here hears the accountant, the townsfolk or the commander. It can go well, or badly."
- **Holy cross:** "A holy cross: first here has an audience with the Lord. He judges them loyal servants (blessed) or heretics (cursed), for 5 turns."
- **Barracks:** "The barracks: first here hires fighters (rogue mercenaries, archers, or an assassin)."
- **Architect:** "The architect: first here has him build something (artillery, a storehouse, or a fortress) anywhere on their side."

When you aim a squad at one, the line under the mouse adds: "…and set off the rumble", "…and the crest", "…and the
mystery", "…and the audience", "…and the holy cross (an audience with the Lord)", "…and the barracks (hire
fighters)", "…and the architect (he builds for you)".

## The mystery square

- **Diviner's cards** (shown as "Name: what it does"):
  - Hero / Maiden / King's Guard: "yours to put in, no crest needed"
  - The Joker: "everything next to it thrown in the air"
  - The Jack: "+2 to each of yours next to it"
  - The King: "+5 to your base"
  - The Queen: "+3 to one of yours"
  - The Ace: "+5 to the squad that found it"
  - The Plague: "all active squads lose -1 health per turn (3 turns)"
- **Thief:** banner "Thief!" / yours: "Pick one enemy squad to steal -3 points"; theirs: "They steal -3 points from one of yours"
- **Plague:** "Plague!" / "All active squads lose -1 health per turn (3 turns)" (theirs: "All their active squads…")
  - Hover on a plagued squad: "The plague: -1 a turn."
- **Trickster:** "Trickster!" / "Every street on the table changes."

## Audience

- Choosing: "Who will you hear?" (enemy: "The enemy hold an audience")
  - The accountant: "Weighs your gold" (enemy: "Weighs their gold")
  - The townsfolk: "Want to hear you" (enemy: "The rebels: Want rallying")
  - The commander: "Advises on tactics"
- **Accountant:** good: "The gold outweighs it." / "Your morale is up, for 5 turns." Bad: "Short." / "Your morale is down, for 5 turns."
- **Townsfolk:** "The townsfolk wait. What do you tell them?" (enemy: "The enemy rally the rebels round the fire"). Good: "They cheer!" (rebels: "The rebels roar!") / "Morale up, and your bases make 1 more a turn, for 5 turns." Bad: "They mutter, and turn away." / "Morale down, and your bases make 1 less a turn, for 2 turns."
- **Commander:** good: "Good advice" / "You see two turns of their moves ahead, for 5 turns." Bad: "Clouded judgement" / "You won't see their moves at all, for 5 turns."

## Holy cross (an audience with the Lord)

- "An audience with the Lord" / "O please, Lord, won't you hear our prayer?!"
- **Blessed:** "Loyal servants!" / "Blessed for 5 turns: anything left to luck or morale goes your way, and your attacks win on a tie."
- **Cursed:** "Heretics!" / "Cursed for 5 turns: anything left to luck or morale goes against you, and attacks on you win on a tie."

## Barracks

- "The barracks! Who will you hire?" (enemy: "The enemy hire fighters at the barracks")
- **Rogue mercenaries:** "Three of 3. They go after the enemy on their own. They don't join up or grow."
- **Archers:** "Two of 2, next to the barracks. They hold there and shoot anyone next to them: 1 a turn."
- **An assassin:** "One of 3. Slips through to their base the safest way, and strikes it: 3, half the time 6."
- Hover on them afterwards:
  - Rogue: "A rogue mercenary: goes after the enemy on its own. Never joins up or grows."
  - Archer: "An archer: holds here, and shoots one enemy next to it each turn, for 1."
  - Assassin: "An assassin: slips through to the enemy base the safest way, and strikes it (3, half the time 6)."
- Assassin's strike: "Assassin!" / "Yours reached their base: -3." (or "a critical hit, -6!")

## Architect

- "Aha!" / "What shall I build you?" (enemy: "He draws up plans for the enemy")
- **Artillery:** "5, dug in at first. Shoots one of theirs next to it for 3 a turn. Never moves."
- **A storehouse:** "3. Does nothing itself, but while it stands your elite are doubled: hero 14, maiden 10, guard 4."
- **A fortress:** "Dug in for good, for 7 turns. Your base or a tower next to it makes 1 more a turn."
- Hover on them afterwards:
  - Artillery: "Artillery: never moves; shoots one enemy next to it each turn, for 3."
  - Storehouse: "A storehouse: while it stands, your elite are doubled."
  - Fortress: "A fortress: can't be attacked. A base or tower of yours next to it makes 1 more a turn."

## Elites (from a crest or a card)

- **Banners when one comes in** (enemy ones say "(enemy)" and "The enemy's…"):
  - Hero / Bastard: "Your elite fighter: 7, for 5 turns. Only their bastard can hurt it."
  - Maiden / Witch: "Your elite: she seduces any of theirs she moves onto (5 or fewer)."
  - King's Guard / Barbarians: "Your elite guards, round your base. Dug in for 2 turns."
- **Hover:**
  - Hero: "7 to deal out. Nothing can attack it. Two streets a turn." (and "Crusted: fixed here." when dug in)
  - Maiden: "Moved onto one of theirs next to her (5 or fewer, or their bastard), she seduces it; or she merges into one of hers, poisoning it."
  - King's Guard: "Holds where it is. Digs in for 2 turns. Yours can stand beside them."
  - Poisoned squad: "Poisoned: attacking, a 50% chance of a critical hit (10 to 60% more)."

## Towers

- Hover: "A tower (yours): makes 2 here for whoever holds it every 4 turns (in N). Walls +1."
- Taken: "Captured!" / "This tower will now produce troops for you." (theirs: "This tower now produces troops for your enemy.")

## The rebels' own moves (not bonuses, but they pop up too)

- **Rally!** "+2 to one of theirs."
- **Sabotage!** "A route is closed (3 turns): nobody can use it." Hover on the route: "Route closed (3 turns)".
