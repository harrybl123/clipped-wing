**The carver's want** (designer): **is Mum all right?** Act 1 left her at the door. It's your first question to anyone up here, and Day 3 is your first chance to find out. Grounded wants the tree; you want your mum; the two pull against each other all chapter.

**The tone** (designer): **you're scared and shocked by everything.** The sky, the light, people you watched die alive, the truth. Shock gets a beat of its own, then the pace picks up.

## What Grounded explains (designer: this is all of it)

Staged like the prologue (designer): narration over black and a few stills, in his voice. **Stills** (suggestion): the two the prologue showed, the tree and the tube, taken back; then the sorting deck, the lowering platform, the capital's lights.

- When humans created AI, it became clear the only choice was to join them or be ruled. Many, generations ago, rejected it, and submitted to living in the shadow of a greater civilisation, to keep what was left of themselves.
- The capital keeps the peace between the integrated and the humans. The king makes the hardest bargain of all: people like you are kept as slaves so that humans aren't destroyed by a greater civilisation. It's important to be useful.
- **What the tree's work is for** (designer): the integrated want carvings, because they maximise efficiency and are superintelligent, so **novelty and imperfection are valuable to them**. Everyone in the tree is encouraged to give different offerings: novelty. *(Suggestion: one line that lands for the carver: they don't want perfect; they can make perfect.)*
- **The shrinking blessings are the world's economics** (designer). *(Suggestion: the capital asks more of the tree for the same; what comes back down is what's left.)*
- The tree is an important economic resource to the king, and now you have power over it: why, and how.
- Why he chose you: he could build a relationship with you from outside the tree, because every piece of your work had the same deficiency. Everyone else's offerings were different every time, so he couldn't make anyone out.
- Why nobody could just go in and say it: everyone is so devout.
- **The canopy's people live off what the tree sends up** (designer, 2026-10-17): the offerings that come up the tube feed and furnish them before the rest goes on, so **they're scavengers really**.
- **He knew it would put you in a difficult position** (the rejections, the hunger blamed on you), **and did it anyway** (designer). The bitten bird was **a child's hunger, something silly** (designer) (suggestion: you meet the child in the mess, who owns up, mortified).
