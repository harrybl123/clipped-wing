in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal. Dialogue here is a sketch; the full draft comes
once the shape is agreed.

## What the design doc asks for
- Morning: you send your reply to message 2 ("Would you like to fly?"), picked from picture options:
  yes (open wings), I can't (a clipped wing), or why? **(doc)**
- Afternoon: Mum is snubbed at the market. Step in and it becomes a short real-time fight with a young
  zealot (dodge, shove, grab; you can't make a proper fist). Win: suspicion jumps. Lose: you're hurt
  and lose food. Hold back: Mum sees it. Then the girl, the one person who talks to you normally, says
  the rot is deeper than they say and asks you to come tonight. **(doc)**
- Evening: message 3 and a real feather, the first proof birds exist. "[Not yet / Neither can I /
  Because I have watched you carve]. I am Grounded. There are many of us beyond the bark." **(doc)**
- Night: the rot, sneaking down to her home deep in the roots. **(doc)**

## Beats (suggestion)

### 1. The summons
Roots folk can't read, so it isn't words: the Tender's summons, a small cloth like the banners hung
round the tree, pushed under the door. Everyone knows what it means. Mum goes pale. "He wants you at
the shrine. Now. Before the rite."

### 2. Your reply, and the hollow bird
- **Choose the reply:** a small screen like the decoding plate, but you scratch it yourself: pick one
  of three pictures (open wings = yes; a clipped wing = I can't; a hook with a dot = why?). Each uses a
  picture he sent you, so your answer is in his own language.
- **Carve a hollow bird:** the carving close-up again, with a new step: **hollowing**, working the knife
  inside the bird without breaking through the wall (a pressure game like smoothing, but stop too hard
  and it cracks: start that step again). The plate goes inside; you seal it.

### 3. The Tender's shrine: the long conversation
The doc: he's "the face of the rules and suspicion; thinks he understands your rejection, and is
wrong". What he wants from this conversation:
1. **Kindness, of a sort.** He's known your family for years. He remembers your mother's birds. He sits
   you on the lone stool under the lamp, facing the elders' empty chairs.
2. **The new arrangement.** "For everyone's sake, you won't come to the morning rite. Bring your
   offering here at dawn; I'll take it up myself. But you will stand on that floor every evening. The
   Tree gives its answer in public, and the tree must see you receive it."
3. **Questions, gently, then not so gently.** "Have you ever opened an offering? Tampered with one?"
   "Have you spoken against the Tree?" "Have you been down to the lower roots?" Your style choices set
   suspicion. One wrong answer and he asks to see your hands.
4. **His theory (wrong).** "The Tree doesn't punish. It prunes. Perhaps it's telling you your work
   belongs elsewhere: not up the tube. Perhaps you're meant for the rot-clearing, or the lamp-oil."
   He offers you a way back: stop carving birds. Carve what you're told.
5. **The stakes.** "The elders are talking. The preacher wants an answer. If it happens again, it won't
   be up to me."
6. **Your bird.** He takes it with the cloth, weighs it in his hand, and pauses: "Lighter than your
   usual." A long moment. Then he lays it in the basket. (The exchange is holy; he'd never open it.)

### 4. The market: Mum snubbed, the fight
- A stallholder refuses to serve Mum; people won't stand near her. A young zealot (the one from Day 3?)
  knocks her basket out of her hands.
- **Step in:** a timing scene for now, like the carving (designer): his swings to dodge, openings to
  shove or grab, timed presses; you can't make a proper fist (a punch does almost nothing). Win:
  suspicion jumps. Lose: you're hurt and lose food. (Could become real-time later.)
- **Hold back:** Mum sees you hold back.

### 5. The girl: why she needs you, in secret
She finds you after the market, and for once she isn't joking.
- **What's happened:** last night the floor of the house next to hers gave way into the rot. The
  Tender's men came, took the family "somewhere safe", and boarded up the hole. Nobody's seen them since.
- **Why she needs you:** the rot is under her house too. She's hidden it. "You're a carver. You know
  wood better than anyone down there. Tell me if my house is going to fall in."
- **Why in secret:** if anyone finds out the rot's under her house, her family will be taken too. And
  if she's seen with the cursed child, people will start asking why. "Come after dark. Don't let
  anyone see you. Not the watch, not the neighbours, nobody."
- (This also sets up Day 6, when she's punished for being kind to you.)

### 6. The evening: the ceremony changes form
Escalating: horror (Day 2) → violence (Day 3) → **ritual** (Day 4). It's organised now, and colder:
- You're not allowed onto the floor. A ring is chalked at the foot of the stairs; you stand in it, alone.
  Mum has to stand with the others.
- When your bird comes back, nobody shouts. The whole crowd turns its back on you, at once, in silence.
  (Days 5 and 6 change form again: the escort; the bitten bird.)
- The Tender announces your house will be watched.

### 7. Home: message 3 and the feather
- Decode message 3: much of it from your glossary. Its first words depend on your reply.
- The feather: a real one, tucked in beside the plate. You've only ever seen carved feathers. (The
  first proof birds exist; the plate's pictures include his symbol, a bird on bare ground.)

### 8. Night: the sneak, her home, the rot
- **The sneak (lower roots at night):** the street has a night watch with lanterns, and neighbours'
  lit windows. Stay out of the light: the high route over the roofs, dark doorways, timing the watch's
  rounds. If the watch sees you, you go back to the start of the street (designer).
- **Her home (new area, not on the map):** tiny, falling apart, crammed with salvaged junk. A board in
  the floor she keeps a rug over.
- **The rot (new area, not on the map):** under her house. Sticky, grimy, dying roots, glowing rot,
  beetles, the hazards from the back routes. Far worse than anyone above admits. Nothing special to
  find there (designer). You look at the wood and you know:
  her house won't hold forever. (No way through yet: the Day 7 roots ending pays this off.)
