## What the design doc asks for
- Morning: you send the fledgling (or your usual bird, the ignore path). **(doc)**

  back route, and you sneak to their window; they promise to come to you tonight. **(doc)**

  lie. **(doc)**
- Every message is another public rejection, costing food and standing. **(doc)**

## Beats (suggestion)

### 1. Carving (home, morning)
A close-up at the bench by firelight, like the prologue, but it's your ruined hand now, older. You make
a few choices, then hold E through each stage:
- **Choices** (flavour, maybe remembered): how small; which wing to clip (the left, like your hand?);
  the eye (open, or half-closed).
- **Stages:** rough out the shape → smooth it → cut the clipped wing → the eye. The grip slips a little
  while you hold (your missing thumb); let go and hold again to steady it.
- The ignore path uses the same scene for your usual bird.

### 2. The morning rite (offering floor)
- Twice the usual crowd, on the stairs, at the archways; people from the market and beyond.
- The crowd parts around you. Whispers: "That's the one." "Cursed."
- The Tender takes your offering with a cloth, and puts it in a basket of its own.
- Across the floor: your friend. He sees you, starts towards you; his parent grabs his arm and pulls
  him out through the archway. If you follow, the parent: "Stay away from my son."
- The girl, leaning on the archway: she asks if you're all right (in her way: dismissive, flirty, but
  she means it). You tell her. She knows the old ways: "His window backs onto the back routes. Come on."

### 3. The back ways (lower roots → back routes)
- She leads you down to the lower roots, shows you a way into the back routes, and leaves you to it
  ("I'm not holding your hand").
- Through the back routes to your friend's back window: crumbling ledges that fall a moment after you
  land, rubble shaking loose from above (warning dust first), a board that gives way. Fail and you drop
  back a level and try again (no deaths yet).
- Later (not now): bugs patrolling loops.

### 4. The window (friend's home)
- He's frightened; his parents are downstairs. You only get a few words before someone calls his name.
- "Not here. Tonight. I'll come to you."

### 5. The evening: the second rejection
Worse than the first (suggestion):
- The crowd is ready for it this time. When your fledgling drops into the basin, nobody screams. They
  turn on you: shouting, something thrown, someone shoves Mum.
- The Tender: no blessings for the carver's house until the Tree is appeased (a food cost).
- Mum, at home: no longer just frightened. Ashamed, angry, or broken (by your dialogue style?).
- The fledgling has been hollowed, and the plate is inside: message 2.

### 6. Night: decoding, then the visit
- Decode message 2: "clipped", "wing", "I", "you" and "fly" are already in your glossary; the new
  pictures are rest, beyond, bark, would/like, and the question.
- The hollow bird shows you how to reply (the reply itself is chosen on Day 4 morning: yes / I can't /
  why?).
- A knock at the window. **Hide the evidence:** a short real-time scramble (the plate, the hollow bird,
  the shavings) before he climbs in. Whatever you don't hide, he may see.
- **Confide or lie.** If he saw something, a lie is harder to make stick.

### The ignore path
- Your usual bird is rejected again with message 1 (still severe).
- The second, very stern choice. Ignore again: "You stayed loyal to the Tree." An ending card, then
  retry from the Day 2 choice.

## To fix (designer, after playtesting)
- The rot drip curtain is too fast to get past: slow the interval.
- Your friend shouldn't shun the girl: roots folk are kind to the lower roots (a bit of scorn, not
  hatred). Soften his reaction at the window. (Both in `docs/ideas.md`.)

- **Lie:** on Day 7 you glimpse him among the torches. **(doc)**
- Your last visits on Day 7 include him only "if things are intact". **(doc)**
- His trust is tracked through the act (Day 2: telling him the truth about his offering raises it).
