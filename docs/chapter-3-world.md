# Chapter 3 world: the capital

The world for [Chapter 3, The Prince](chapter-3-design.md), built the way Act 1 and Chapter 2 were: the world first (area briefs, the map, then each area as data with placeholder art), then the cast, then the story day by day.

Labels: **(doc)** comes from the chapter design, **(designer)** was decided in conversation, and **(suggestion)** is Claude's proposal, kept until you confirm or change it. Names are placeholders for now (designer).

## The idea

- One of the last human cities: what's left of society is mostly governance. It manages places like the tree, and trades with the integrated. **(doc)**
- Height means status, as in the tree: the citadel at the top, the city below it, the low district at the bottom. **(doc)**
- A futurist city with a castle feel. **(doc)** Obviously futuristic, and much more cyberpunk than anything so far, while keeping this world's earthiness. **(designer)**
- The tree is on the horizon from the prince's balcony. **(doc)** In Chapter 4, the canopy's lookout sees the capital's lights. **(doc)**
- The dramatic irony **(suggestion)**: the capital's blue glow is the same light as the tube. A player who's played the first two chapters might see that the city runs on the same thing as the tree; nobody here says where the power comes from. When the power's cut, the glow goes out.

## Look

- **Clean marble, gold, and a blue glow.** **(designer)**
  - **Marble** (the palette's white and its shades): the citadel's walls, floors and columns, polished, cool. The citadel is to the city what the Canopy is to the tree: pale and high. **(suggestion)**
  - **Gold** (amber's shades): inlay in the marble, trims, fittings, the citadel's wealth. Amber also stays the colour of people: lamps in homes, food stalls, fires. **(suggestion)**
  - **The blue glow** (cyan and its shades): power. Light runs in seams through the marble, in strips along the streets, in signs and screens. The more power a place has, the more it glows. **(suggestion)**
- **Cyberpunk** **(suggestion)**:
  - Screens everywhere, on walls, over streets, on the lifts: news, notices, and your father's face when he speaks.
  - Signs in cyan and amber light, flickering where the power's weak.
  - Cables everywhere below the citadel: bundled down the towers, slung across the low district's streets like a ceiling.
  - Glass lifts running up the face of the city.
  - Rain at night in the city, and wet streets reflecting the signs.
  - Steam from vents and food stalls.
- **The earthiness** **(suggestion)**: the marble is stone, old and worn smooth where people walk; the citadel was a castle before it was anything else. Below, people live by hand: patched homes, cooking, washing lines between the cables, plants in pots, things mended and reused. The glasshouse is the only real greenery. **(doc)** No chrome: metal is gold or dark and worn.
- **The low district is darker** **(suggestion)**: less power, so fewer glowing seams and more amber from lamps and fires. Where the power's been cut, nothing glows at all.
- **Red stays reserved**, and copper (the scavenger's). **The prince's reserved shade is wine** (designer): a deep, royal red, on his collar. Shades of wine follow the same rule as red.
- **No integrated in the capital** (designer): nothing of theirs is seen here.
- **Time of day** **(suggestion)**:
  - morning: a clear, pale sky, the marble bright
  - afternoon: hazy white, the city's glow faint
  - evening: amber dusk, the glow coming up
  - night: dark sky, the city lit in cyan and amber, rain below

## The prince

- Young and attractive, with a shaved head: dark hair, cut very short. Not balding, not old. **(designer)**
- **(suggestion)** Clothes: a fitted, high-collared coat, dark (so he stands out against the pale marble), with gold down its front and at the cuffs; the collar and a sash at the waist in wine. Simpler and plainer when he goes down into the city with his hand.
- Drawn in colour, like the scavenger (not a rim-lit silhouette like the tree's people). **(suggestion)**
- Tan skin (designer: Claude's choice).
- **Wine** is his reserved colour (designer), on his collar and sash. **(suggestion: where)**

## The map

**(suggestion)** A side-on view of the city climbing towards the citadel, like a cutaway: the same controls as the other maps.
- **The citadel** crowns the top: marble towers and gold, its rooms shown as lit windows (like the tree's map). The sealed wing is a dark tower with a faint hum of light.
- **The city** steps down below it in tiers, with the lifts as glowing lines up its face.
- **The low district** at the bottom, under the cables, in shadow.
- **The gate** at ground level on one side, where the road comes in from outside.
- **The tree** small on the far horizon.
- **The power on the map:** districts whose power is cut go dark on the map too, so you can see the pushbacks spread across the city.

## What the world has to show (from the story)

- **Power cut where the symbol is:** whole streets go dark (their glow, their signs, their screens), and come back. **(designer's pushback)**
- **The symbol:** chalked on walls, spreading, scrubbed off, back again. **(doc)**
- **Your father on every screen**, calling them terrorists, and at the end, your own broadcast. **(designer's pushbacks)**
- **The sealed wing's hum** when your father is in there. **(doc)**
- **The raid:** the hideout ransacked. **(designer)**
- **Guests who can't leave:** the guest wing's doors. **(designer)**

## New things to build (suggestion)

- **Wall styles:** `marble` for the citadel (panels, gold inlay, glowing seams); the city's building fronts and the low district's patched walls.
- **An outdoor backdrop for the city:** towers with lit windows, signs, cables, the citadel above, the tree far off; rain at night.
- **Lights and props that come and go with the story** (today they're painted into the backdrop once): for power cuts and the symbol.
- **Screens:** a feature for a wall screen that shows a face or words, which the story changes.
- **Flickering signs.**
- **A stone floor** for footsteps, and the city's sounds (rain, traffic of people, the hum of power).
- **The prince** as the player (`Player.look`), his sprite, portrait and voice; **a third map** (`WORLDS`).
- The lifts can be the trunk climb's moving platforms.

## Build order (suggestion)

1. The map, every place dark. **Done** (`src/data/capitalMap.ts`, `src/ui/paintCapitalMap.ts`): the city side-on at night, climbing to the citadel; halls, the lifts and streets between places.
2. Your quarters: the first area sets the look (marble, gold, the glow; the balcony over the city and the tree on the horizon). **Built** (`src/data/areas/capital/princeQuarters.ts`; dev `?area=prince-quarters`): `walls: 'marble'`, and `views`, windows and open sides onto the city painted for the time of day (`src/ui/paintCityView.ts`; the room dims from dusk).
3. The rest of the citadel. **Built**, every room (placeholder art, no people yet; files in `src/data/areas/capital/`, props in `citadelProps.ts`):
   - the halls (`?area=citadel-halls`): two floors and a grand stair, doors to every room, banners, statues, windows over the city;
   - the audience hall (`?area=audience-hall`): columns, benches, the rope, the dais with the throne (gold-backed) and your seat beside it under the great screen; doors to the halls and the lifts;
   - the council chamber (`?area=council-chamber`): tiers of seats, their lights dark where nobody sits, your father's at the top; the table of light, the city in blue with its cut districts dark;
   - your father's study (`?area=fathers-study`): dark wood panelling (`walls: 'panelled'`), bookshelves, his desk by the window, a clock; the door to the sealed wing;
   - the sealed wing (`?area=sealed-wing`): a corridor that gets plainer, the gold running out, to the seamless door glowing at its edge; a loud hum;
   - the glasshouse (`?area=glasshouse`): a glass roof (sky only), a gold rib arching across it, vines, beds, small trees, the fountain, the terrace;
   - the banquet gallery (`?area=banquet-gallery`): the dance floor and its screen, the long table with every place empty, your father's chair at its head, a balcony over the city;
   - the diplomat's office (`?area=diplomats-office`): the wall of maps with threads between the pins, letters, his desk, a screen, his private door down to the lifts;
   - the guest wing (`?area=guest-wing`): shut doors along a gold corridor, windows that don't open, your friend's room open at the far end, the guards' barrier by the way out.
   - **Each room's own thing, and motion** (designer: more creative stuff in the citadel; Claude's choices): brass message tubes along the halls' ceilings, capsules shooting through them and down into the diplomat's door, and one dropping into his desk (an echo of the tree's tube); a glass lift between the halls' floors; the old castle's stone gate round the audience hall's great doors, its iron portcullis raised for good, and light running along the gold in the floor to the throne; chandeliers; screens that scroll; the council's table of light flickering, a scan sweeping it; the castle's own hearth in your father's study, the only real fire in the citadel, a case clock with its pendulum swinging, a model of the city under glass; cables along the sealed wing's ceiling and floor with light running along them into the door; the glasshouse's fountain running and the glass dripping; the guards' screens watching the guest wing's doors; a telescope on your balcony, pointed at the tree. New area features: `frames` (an animated prop) and `pulse` (something travelling along a line); `swing` can be `quiet` with a `ropeColour`.
   - **Each room its own character** (designer: more detail; lots of rooms have the same things): the grand marble rooms have a moulded cornice and a dado along every floor (a gold rail, inset panels), with gold inlay here and there; the council chamber is black marble veined with gold (`walls: 'darkMarble'`), lamps hanging low over the table on long chains, no banners or light strips; the sealed wing is bare white panels with nothing on them (`walls: 'bare'`), only the cables; the diplomat's office is warm plaster over a dark wood dado (`walls: 'plaster'`), notes pinned up, brass filing drawers; the banquet gallery is a hall of mirrors with a musicians' gallery (a harp, a drum) instead of a screen; the guest wing's windows are behind gold lattice, and empty gilded cages hang along it; your quarters are a young man's: a canopied bed, a rack of practice blades and a sparring dummy, a game of rule left mid-game, curtains at the balcony; the halls have a gallery of past rulers' portraits along the upper floor; the glasshouse has trellises with plants climbing them.
- The prince as the player is **done**: sprite, portrait, voice (`PRINCE`, `PRINCE_PORTRAIT`, `prince` in `VOICES`), used in every capital area.
4. The city: the promenade first (the outdoor look), then the lifts, the low district, the hideout, the data centre, the gate. **Built** (placeholder art, no people yet; props in `src/data/areas/capital/cityProps.ts`):
   - the city's outdoor look: `outdoor.backdrop: 'city'` (the capital's towers in two rows rising towards the citadel, which stands above them in marble and gold, its sealed wing the tallest; pale stone by day, from dusk dark with windows lit cyan and amber, strips of blue light, signs, cables; `citadelX`, `cityFrom`), `outdoor.ground: 'street'` (paving, a line of blue light in the kerb at night), and `rain` (a new feature, at night);
   - the promenade (`?area=promenade`): shopfronts under striped awnings with the buildings over them, signs flickering, the square with its great screen on a mast, food stalls and a vent steaming, benches, lamps, a raised walkway with shops along it, cables overhead; exits to the lifts, the gate (an alley) and the low district;
   - the lifts (`?area=lifts`, three screens high): a tower's face, stations at the citadel, the promenade and the data centre, the street at the bottom with the operator's booth; two glass shafts, the cars taking you a stage at a time;
   - the low district (`?area=low-district`): homes stacked and patched both sides and behind, walkways along them with stairs, washing, plants in pots, a dead screen, a cooking fire and steam, cables everywhere; the low doorway down to the hideout;
   - the hideout (`?area=hideout`, inside the low district, `INSIDE_AREAS`): an old stone cellar (`walls: 'stone'`) made a bar: the counter, bottles, a long table with benches, barrels and crates, notices pasted up, lamps in cages; the back way out;
   - the data centre (`?area=data-centre`): bare panels in cold blue, rows of racks blinking, the operator's desk and screens, cables with light running along them into a sealed door like the citadel's;
   - the gate (`?area=city-gate`): inside, the city's buildings and the checkpoint (booth, barrier, screen); the wall, old stone and dull gold, with the passage through it; outside, the road across the waste and the tree on the horizon.
   - **Not yet:** the symbol on the walls (what it looks like is the designer's to say), and the raid's aftermath in the hideout.
5. The cast: the prince as the player first, then everyone else, placed in each area by time of day.
