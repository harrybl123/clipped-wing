## Purpose
Their family's house, with the back window. **(doc)** Your closest friend since childhood: devout,
believes completely, dreams of rising. Their family forbids them from seeing you. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Day 3: the family has forbidden you from coming near; the girl shows you a back route and you
sneak to their window. **(doc)** Day 1 (you can visit everyone) and Day 7 (a last visit, if things
are intact). **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A devout family, a little better off than yours and much tidier: matching stools, a table
runner, a proper bed, a fuller food store, amber oil lamps rather than fungus jars. The household
shrine is the centrepiece. Their one piece of tech isn't used, as it is in your home: it's
a relic, glowing cyan in the shrine.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen, two levels. Ground floor: front door from the market, the family table with a plate
shelf, the shrine under a painted tube mural rising to a pale Canopy, flanked by banners; the
hearth and the store under the landing. Stairs with a handrail up to your friend's room: chest,
drawings pinned to the wall, a cloak on a peg, the bed, and the back window, which opens onto a
crawlway glowing with fungi (the way to the back routes).

## Key props
The shrine with the relic, the mural, the drawings (the tube, someone rising, the Canopy), the
back window. **(suggestion)**

## People

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to your friend and a parent; look at the shrine, the store and the drawings. Placeholder
text only, in the tree's faith: rising means going to the Canopy, their heaven. **(designer)**
