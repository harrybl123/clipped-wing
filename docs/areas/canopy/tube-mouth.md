## Purpose
Where offerings and the ascended arrive (doc). Up the tube, where the chapter starts: you come to here, under the sky for the first time, and the master is kneeling by you (designer).

## When it's visited
Day 1 dawn (up the tube). Passed through after; offerings arriving each morning (suggestion).

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Sets the canopy's look. The tentacles (doc: tubes out of the top of the tree like an octopus's tentacles) rising and curling over a wide deck, cyan light pulsing up inside them; open sky above, leaves all round, the great house beyond. Wind.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Two screens wide, a little tall. A broad living-wood deck round the tentacles' bases; the mouth you arrive from (a tentacle's open end, a basin under it, padded with old cloth); catwalks up among the tentacles; the way to the great house.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the tentacles, glowing, swaying very slightly.
- The basin where offerings land, a cable basket carrying them on to the sorting deck; the day's offerings in it (the roots' ornaments, the upper branches' masterwork).
- Old cushions where the ascended land (doc: they arrive here too).

## People
The master at dawn on Day 1 (designer). Sorters collecting the morning's offerings (suggestion).

## Interactions (suggestion)
The master; the basin (Look: today's offerings, things you know); the tentacles (Look).

## Exits (suggestion)
The great house; the walkways.
