## Purpose
Everyone who ever went up the tube is alive here (doc). The ascended are mostly the revolution's core (doc). A shock you walk through on Day 1, on both routes (suggestion).

## When it's visited
Day 1 morning (on the way to Grounded); after, a place to talk to the ascended.

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A long hall in the boughs, quiet; daylight through high windows. Not a heaven: an old people's home and a guild hall at once. On the walls, every master's last piece, the ones they carried up, each with the spiral (Act 1: every master cuts it into their last piece).

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One and a half screens wide: a hall of benches and small rooms, a terrace at the end.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the wall of last pieces, every one with the spiral.
- Names you know from the tree's stories, alive.

## People
The ascended (doc), old, at work or resting.

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to the ascended (each says something about going up, and what they found); Look at the last pieces.

## Exits (suggestion)
The walkways.
