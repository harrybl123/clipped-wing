Open air and wind, bridges swaying, the drop below into leaves and mist. Gardens on the platforms, planters on the rails, beehives, water funnels, a turbine turning. Offerings everywhere: carved birds along the rails (designer: good suggestion).

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Three screens wide, several levels: the hub of the canopy, with doors to most places. Bridges sway (the `swing` feature's ropes); a pulley lift between levels (a `mover`).

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** a rail lined with carved birds, other people's, bare wood.
- Gardens, beehives, solar sails, water funnels, cable baskets crossing overhead.

## People
Canopy folk by day; lanterns and a few people at night.
