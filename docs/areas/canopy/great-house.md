# Area brief: the great house

Chapter 4. Not built yet. Names are placeholders. World: [chapter-4-world.md](../../chapter-4-world.md).

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The grand central treehouse (doc): the sorting deck (offerings sorted into what's kept and what leaves), the blessing workshop (where blessings are actually made, by people), the mess and most people's homes (doc). Where the truth is plainest.

## When it's visited
Day 1 afternoon (the colony, the mess); Day 2 night (after battle 1, the hoard); Day 3 morning (the workshop, the delivery); Day 4 (getting ready).

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Busy and warm. Living wood, woven walls, carved panels from the tree's offerings set into them; daylight through leaf-shaped solar sails over the top deck; lanterns at night.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
A cutaway, three floors, two screens tall:
- **Top: the sorting deck.** The cable basket from the tube mouth; tables where sorters split each offering: kept (bins for the colony) or on (crates, stencilled, for the lowering platform).
- **Middle: the blessing workshop.** Benches; masters at work making blessings to order; the shapes the tree knows, turned out by hand; the master's bench with her bottle.
- **Bottom: the mess and homes.** Long tables, a kitchen, the portions; doors to homes along a gallery.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the sorting tables: one pile kept, one crated away.
- Blessings half made on the benches, every one with a spiral hidden in it if it's the master's (doc).
- The mess's furniture is offerings: a master's chair, a carved screen, bowls from the roots.

## People
Sorters, the ascended at the benches, the master, the quartermaster (she runs the stores and the mess), the doubter, the hatch-keeper, canopy-born families, the child who bit your bird (designer), the messenger passing through.

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk round; Look at the piles, the blessings, the portions.

## Exits (suggestion)
The tube mouth; the walkways; the message loft (above the workshop).
