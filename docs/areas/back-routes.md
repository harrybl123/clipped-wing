# Area brief: the back routes

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/backRoutes.ts`, `story/back-routes.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Hidden crawlways the girl shows you. **(doc)** Day 3: a way to your friend's back window. Day 6: tail
the Tender to a meeting with the preacher and elders and overhear them. Day 7: move as a ghost,
because you're recognised everywhere. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Days 3, 6, 7. **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Hidden, cramped, darker and damper than anywhere: close to the rot. Soft wood, drips, fungi
thickest where the wood is worst. Light leaks down from the places above: amber through the
shrine's grate, the tube's cyan through a grate under the offering floor (with dust drifting in
it), amber and a flicker of colour through a crack under the market. A heap of things nobody
wanted: broken blessings, a cracked cell that barely glows.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
960×400, the camera follows you. A winding network on three levels (tunnels 40px tall, just enough
to walk hunched; no crouching yet **(designer)**), joined by shafts with boards to jump up and drop
through, plus a dead-end crack and a side chamber. Heartwood between the tunnels is dense packed
wood (`heartwood: 'packed'`). The girl's glowing marks (arrows, a spiral, a cross for "not this
way") are at the turnings.

## Exits
Your home (through a new gap at the foot of its wall by the rot), the lower roots, your friend's
back window, the Tender's shrine (under its grate; not built yet) and the offering floor (under
its grate). **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Listen at the shrine's grate, look up through the offering floor's grate, search the broken
blessings, look at one of her marks. Placeholder text only.
