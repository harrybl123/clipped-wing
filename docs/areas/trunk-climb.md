# Area brief: the trunk climb

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/trunkClimb.ts`, `story/trunk-climb.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Outer shafts and ledges. **(doc)** Night 1: the platformer tutorial up the trunk to watch the
Ascension lights; teaches movement and your weak grip. Day 7: the chase. **(doc)**

Why anyone comes here: it's the only unguarded way to the archive. **(designer)** For
roots-dwellers it's the back way up; climbers use it on Ascension nights to watch the lights, and
a metal ladder at the top leads to a hatch in the back of the archive, which gives the Day 5
heist (no ally) its way in. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A tall shaft up the inside of the trunk with the tube rising the whole height behind a lattice
of roots, dust drifting up it. Climbing moves from green into gold: glowing fungus shelves at the
bottom, then bark ledges, climbers' planks on struts with lamps hung from ropes, then scavenged
metal ledges and a cell lantern near the top. Knotholes, handholds, climbers' marks (one figure
per climber who made it), a rest nook with a blanket.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
480×810 (three screens high); the camera follows you. Ledges zigzag up, each within a jump of the
last. Moving platforms **(designer)**: climbers' rigging partway up, a plank sliding along a rope
and then a lift on a pulley. At the top, a lookout by the tube.

## Exits
Bottom → the Tender's shrine. Top left → the middle tiers. The metal ladder → the archive (its
back hatch). Neither is built yet. **(suggestion)**
