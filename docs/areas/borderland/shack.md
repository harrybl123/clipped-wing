# Area brief: your shack

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/shack.ts`, `story/borderland/shack.ink`; dev `?area=shack`). Your brother and the dog come with the cast.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Home with your brother. The dog is hidden under the floor. **(doc)** This chapter's version of Act 1's home: where days start and end, and where the dog has to be kept secret. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Every morning and night. Visitors are the danger: your brother can't keep a secret **(doc)**, so anyone at the door is a risk to the dog. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Small, cramped, kept tidy by someone who's trying. Walls of mismatched panels: a curved hull plate, a slab of dark glass as a window shutter, sheet metal riveted over gaps. A small fire in a cut-down casing gives the amber light. One dead part on a shelf still glows faint cyan, and your brother treats it as a night light.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen, one floor with a sleeping loft reached by a ladder.
- Ground floor: the door, the fire, a table made from a hatch, the floorboards over the dog's hiding hole.
- Loft: your brother's bed and yours, and the hole in the roof where the light comes in.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the loose floor panel over the dog's hole, with a gap where a nose can poke out.
- Your scavenging kit by the door: a hook, a sack and a pry bar.
- Your brother's collection of small finds, lined up by size.
- A tally of rations scratched on the wall.

## People
- Your brother: adores the dog and can't keep a secret. **(doc)**
- The dog, under the floor. **(doc)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Your brother (talk), the floor panel (the dog), the fire, your kit, his collection, and bed (ends the day).

## Exits (suggestion)
The door to the settlement. On the map, the shack sits on the settlement's outer edge, next to the swap yard and the scrap stacks.

## Open questions
- How big is the dog's hiding place? Can the dog get out by itself?
- Where are the parents, and does the shack show it (an empty bed, their things)?
