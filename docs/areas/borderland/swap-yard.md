# Area brief: the swap yard

Chapter 2. **(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The market: finds for food. **(doc)** This chapter's roots market. It shows that finds are the currency. **(doc, suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Mornings and afternoons; it packs up at dusk. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Loud, crowded and haggling. Stalls are made from upturned casings, and awnings from stretched cable-mesh and cloth. The food is poor: roots, dried things, a pot of something. The scrap on the stall tables is beautiful and nobody cares. Daylight; braziers at the end of the day.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Wider than a screen (the camera follows).
- A row of stalls along the ground.
- A stack of crates you can climb onto, which gives a view over the crowd.
- The fence's stall, half hidden in an alcove at the far end.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the food stall's scales, where the price of a meal is weighed out in scrap.
- Hanging cables strung with small finds.
- A board of prices drawn in scavenger signs.

## People
- The fence: buys what you shouldn't have. **(doc)**
- Food sellers, scrap traders and a crowd.

## Interactions (suggestion)
Trade at the food stall, talk to the fence and traders, look at the price board.

## Exits (suggestion)
To your shack, the tally house, the scrap stacks and the picked fields (the settlement's way out to the grounds).

## Open questions
- Are the food prices set by the boss? (Rations handed out as favours **(doc)** suggests he controls food.)
