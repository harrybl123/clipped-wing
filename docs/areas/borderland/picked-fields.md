# Area brief: the picked fields

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/pickedFields.ts`, `story/borderland/picked-fields.ink`; dev `?area=picked-fields`). People come with the cast.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Scavenged clean; where everyone works. **(doc)** Where the chapter starts, and where the dog's nose first pays off. It is the first open sky in the game, with the tree on the horizon. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Every working day, mornings and afternoons. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A wide, flat waste of dug-over dirt. Scavengers bend over holes. The scrap that's left is what nobody could shift or sell: a hull too big to move, a mast bent double. Pale dusty sky; the tree vast on the horizon, its top lost in white. Hard, tired, hot.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Two screens wide, outdoors.
- Low mounds and holes.
- A half-buried hull you can climb.
- Spoil heaps.
- A track back to the settlement at one end, and on into the grounds at the other.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the stuck hull: the curved back of something enormous, half buried, picked of every panel that could be pried off, too big to ever dig out.
- Dug holes, spoil heaps, a bent mast, coils of cable, cracked glass slabs.
- One part that still glows faint cyan where nobody can reach it.

## People
- Scavengers at work, the old scavenger. **(suggestion)**
- Your friend works here. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Dig (later: the dog finds what others can't), the old scavenger, look at the hull and the glowing part.

## Exits (suggestion)
To the swap yard (the settlement), the sunk town, the dog's hollow and the tree's edge.
