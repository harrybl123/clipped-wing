## Mood and light (suggestion)
On a rise at the top of the settlement, walled with the best scrap: whole panels, matched, cleaned. Inside, it's wealth by the borderland's standards: real food on a table, a working light (a part that still glows cyan steadily, the only one in town), rugs. The boss's warmth, with guards at every door.
