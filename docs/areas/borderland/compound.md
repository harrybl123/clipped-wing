## Purpose
The boss's home and seat. Off limits at first; it opens once you're brought in. **(doc)** If you move your brother here (big choice 1), he lives here from then on. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Closed until the rise; after that, any time. **(doc, suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
On a rise at the top of the settlement, walled with the best scrap: whole panels, matched, cleaned. Inside, it's wealth by the borderland's standards: real food on a table, a working light (a part that still glows cyan steadily, the only one in town), rugs. The boss's warmth, with guards at every door.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
- **The compound (outside):** the lane, with a stool where the gate guard sits; the gateway, the wall cut across your path (two thick posts, a lintel with the clan's ring, the leaves swung open), which you walk through; the yard, with washing on a line, a lamp post, the water tank and the tribute under its cloth; then the house front: two storeys of whole matched panels, a string of small lamps under the eaves, windows with curtains and lamplight, striped awnings and boxes of cloth flowers under the lower ones, a bench with cushions, and the porch with lamps over the open front door.
- **The house (inside), an area within the compound:** not on the map (the map shows you at the compound). Walls of whole matched panels (`walls: 'matched'`).
  - **Downstairs:** the hall (pegs with cloaks, boots), the main room (the long table with real food, under the working light), the kitchen (a stove going, a pot on it, shelves of food put by, pots on a rail, a water barrel), and the circle's side room (stools, a board of who owes what, the locked door).
  - **Upstairs, up the stairs at the right:** the boss's room (a big bed with a patchwork quilt, a chest, a wash stand, a rail of clothes), a sitting room (cushions, a low table, a shelf of prized finds, one still glowing), a room with two beds, and windows over the settlement. The walkway door to the tally house is up here.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the working light. The only part in the borderland that still does what it was made to do, and the boss keeps it.
- The table where the circle eats.
- A locked room nobody enters (the vanished girl's? the daughter's?).
