**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The border with another clan's turf. **(doc)** Where you catch the rival scout (big choice 2), and where crossing over begins if you befriend them. **(doc, suggestion)**

## When it's visited

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A line of junk fence across the waste, half markers and half wall: poles topped with clan signs, wire, piled plates. Your side is picked bare. Their side is no different, but you're not allowed on it. At night their fires burn amber in the distance.

- A dip where people cross unseen.
- Their side beyond.

## Key props (suggestion)

- The watch post, and the gaps in the fence.
