# Area brief: the deep ground

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/deepGround.ts`, `story/borderland/deep-ground.ink`; dev `?area=deep-ground`). `outdoor.ground: 'deep'`: hard dark ground, grooves converging on the horizon, notches where they cross your path. The tree small behind you.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Forbidden and far out. **(doc)** The edge of what the borderland knows. Somewhere you're not meant to go. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Late in the chapter, if at all. Maybe where you leave at the end. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Past the last junk, the ground changes: harder, darker, cut through with straight lines that run off to the horizon, like the marks of something enormous. No people. The tree is a long way behind you. The air is still.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Two screens wide, outdoors, mostly empty: a long walk to a point where you stop.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the straight lines in the ground running away to the horizon. Nobody knows what made them.
- A last marker: the clan's sign that says go no further.
- A scavenger's sled left loaded in a groove before the marker, its pull rope lying ahead: somebody went out and didn't bring it back. Nobody has taken from it.
- Past the marker, an older marker lying flat: the line has been pulled back over the years.
- Beyond that, a row of tall smooth posts beside the grooves, all the same height; one still has a faint cyan light at its tip.
