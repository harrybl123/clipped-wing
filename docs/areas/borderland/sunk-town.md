# Area brief: the sunk town

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/sunkTown.ts`, `story/borderland/sunk-town.ink`; dev `?area=sunk-town`). Rebuilt the same day, deeper (designer: more underground, deeper, more rooms, more to find, more detail in each room; no falling debris).

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Half-buried ruins of the old society: rich but unstable. **(doc)** The best finds and the most danger. The clearest sign that people lived out here once, in ways nobody now understands. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
When you dare. Later, the raids may hit here. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The tops of buildings stick out of the ground: smooth walls with no joins, rounded windows, a staircase that goes down into the dirt. Inside, dark rooms half full of earth, and the faint cyan of things still powered. Silent. It's the strangest place in the borderland, and nothing in it is explained.

## Size and layout
Three levels below the surface, darker and stranger the deeper you go, with more to find the deeper you go. **(designer)** The rooms are **(suggestion)**:
- **The surface:** domes and a tipped tower top in the dirt, stairs into the ground, a broken window (the way into level 1), and the mouth of a deep stairwell with a scavenger's rope tied to a stake beside it.
- **The stairwell:** a smooth shaft down past every level. Its flights have fallen, leaving scars on the walls; you climb the fallen slabs. The rope hangs down the middle, knotted, and stops short of the bottom.
- **Level 1:** the screen room (two dead screens, seats facing them, windows full of earth, a hanging ceiling panel, a chalk arrow to the door), the door that opens by itself, and the long room (empty shelves, a dead strip light with one cell lit, a crack along the ceiling, chalk tallies by the stairwell).
- **Level 2:** the hall of boxes (walls of identical sealed boxes, a few prised open, two still lit; the floor that gave way into level 3 was taken out (designer: clunky and awkward); level 3 is down the stairwell), a room filled with earth to the ceiling with a crawl dug through it and shored with bent plates (crouch to get through), and beyond it someone's old camp (a blanket, a cup, a dead lamp, a broken digging tool, the walls covered in chalk).
- **Level 3:** a round tunnel with a rail and a carriage lying in it, and at the end the bright room: clean, no dirt at all, with a glass column full of cyan light, still on and humming.
- **No falling debris.** **(designer)** The danger is the crawl, and what's camped down there.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** a doorway that still opens by itself when you come near, then stops halfway, juddering.
- Rounded windows, stairs into the dirt, things on walls that might be screens.
- Signs of the scavengers who came before: the rope, chalk arrows and tallies, a circle crossed through, the dug crawl, the camp.
- The bright room's column, the deepest and strongest cyan in the borderland. It hums with the tube's sound from Act 1. **(designer: keep it, a good link)**

## People
- None by default. Maybe a rival scavenger. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Look at the dome, the stairs, the rope, the screen, the door, the tallies, the crawl, the chalk and the column. Search six places, one find each (draft): under the seats (a ring of pale casing), the shelves (fine coloured wire), the boxes (a glass slab that lights when warm), the camp (grey cloth that won't tear), the carriage (a cylinder that ticks), the column's slot (something small and warm). Finds aren't kept yet; they'll become items when Chapter 2's finds and tally are built.

## Exits (suggestion)
To the picked fields and the deep ground.
