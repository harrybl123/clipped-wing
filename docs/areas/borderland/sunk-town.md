# Area brief: the sunk town

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/sunkTown.ts`, `story/borderland/sunk-town.ink`; dev `?area=sunk-town`). Surface domes, a broken window down into two buried rooms, the self-opening door (`autodoor` feature), falling debris and a collapsing ceiling (Act 1's `rubble` and `collapse`; their lumps are still wooden), a climb back up.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Half-buried ruins of the old society: rich but unstable. **(doc)** The best finds and the most danger. The clearest sign that people lived out here once, in ways nobody now understands. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
When you dare. Later, the raids may hit here. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The tops of buildings stick out of the ground: smooth walls with no joins, rounded windows, a staircase that goes down into the dirt. Inside, dark rooms half full of earth, and the faint cyan of things still powered. Silent. It's the strangest place in the borderland, and nothing in it is explained.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Wider and deeper than a screen.
- Rooftops at ground level.
- A way down through a broken window into rooms beneath.
- Floors at angles.
- Collapsing ceilings (hazards like Act 1's collapse and rubble).

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** a doorway that still opens by itself when you come near, then stops halfway, juddering.
- Rounded windows, stairs into the dirt, things on walls that might be screens.

## People
- None by default. Maybe a rival scavenger. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Search rooms, look at the walls, and the door.

## Exits (suggestion)
To the picked fields and the deep ground.

## Open questions
- Should anything here hint at who built it (writing, pictures), or keep it blank?
- How dangerous is it, and can you die here or only get knocked back?
