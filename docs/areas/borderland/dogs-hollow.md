# Area brief: the dog's hollow

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/dogsHollow.ts`, `story/borderland/dogs-hollow.ink`; dev `?area=dogs-hollow`). Hidden on the map, and its path out of the picked fields hidden, until `found_dog` (designer). The dog comes with the cast.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Your secret training spot on the outskirts. **(doc)** Where you teach the dog to find things, and where you can be yourself. It's at risk once someone follows you. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
When you train the dog, usually early or late, when nobody's about. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A dip in the ground, out of sight, sheltered by a toppled structure that leans over it like a roof. Quieter than anywhere else; the wind drops. A few tough plants. The tree on the horizon.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen, outdoors.
- The dip.
- The leaning shelter.
- A rim you can watch from.
- A path in that only you use.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the leaning shelter, with the dog's bed and your training things (finds buried and dug up again).
- Marks in the dirt where you've buried things for practice.

## People
- You and the dog. **(doc)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Train the dog (bury a find, the dog finds it) and rest.

## Exits (suggestion)
To the picked fields.

## Open questions
- Is training a minigame?
