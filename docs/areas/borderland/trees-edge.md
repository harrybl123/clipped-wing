## Purpose
Where the borderland meets the tree's roots. Nobody goes close. **(doc)** The tree's mystery, seen from outside. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Rarely, maybe once. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The roots come out of the ground like walls, bigger than anything in the settlement. The bark is dark and wet. In the cracks between the roots, the green glow of fungus, the only green in the borderland. Above, the trunk goes up into white. It's quiet, and something in the wood creaks and settles, the sound of the tree from Act 1, heard from outside.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One and a half screens, outdoors.
- The waste running up to the roots.

- A crack between two roots that's dark inside, which you can't go into.

## Key props (suggestion)
