# Area brief: the bar

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/bar.ts`, `story/borderland/bar.ink`; dev `?area=bar`). Doors: the lane to the compound, down to the gambling den, and through to the brothel from the gallery. The barkeep and the crews come with the cast; overheard talk (barks) needs them.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The bar or club where crews gather. Gossip and suspicion spread here. **(doc)** It's where you hear what the town thinks of you, which gets worse as the raids land, like Act 1's crowds turning day by day. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Evenings and nights. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Warm, smoky and loud. A bar counter made from a long curved panel. The lights are dead tech that the barkeep has rigged to burn oil, so they glow amber through old cyan glass. There's music of some kind, and crews at their own tables.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen and a bit, two floors.
- Ground: the counter, crew tables and a stage or corner for music.
- Upstairs: a gallery and booths where quieter deals happen. You can overhear from the stairs.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the counter, a single smooth curved panel with no joins, polished by elbows.
- Crew marks scratched into each table.
- Bottles made from odd glass.

## People
- The barkeep. **(doc)**
- Crews, your friend and the gambler at times. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to the barkeep, listen in at tables (barks over heads, as in Act 1), go up to the booths.

## Exits (suggestion)
To the settlement: the gambling den and the brothel are next door.

## Open questions
- Is it a bar, a club, or both? Is there music or dancing?
