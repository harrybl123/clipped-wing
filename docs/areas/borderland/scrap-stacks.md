# Area brief: the scrap stacks

Chapter 2. **(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Junk towers between the shacks, for climbing and hiding. **(doc)** This chapter's back routes: a way across the settlement over the top, out of sight, and where the kid lookout watches from. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Any time. Climbing to follow someone, hide or overhear. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Towers of compacted junk two or three screens high, leaning on each other: hull plates, frames, cables, smashed casings. Gaps to crawl through, ledges to jump. From the top, the tree on the horizon and the whole settlement below. Some loose stacks shift under you.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Taller than a screen.
- Ledges zigzag up between the towers.
- Bridges of cable.
- A lookout's perch at the top.
- Crawlways through the stacks lead to other parts of the settlement.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the lookout's perch, with a view of the tree. Somebody has made it comfortable.
- Loose plates that shift (hazards, like Act 1's crumbling boards).

## People
- The kid lookout. **(doc)**
- Your brother sometimes, with him. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
The kid lookout, the view from the perch, and crawlways.

## Exits (suggestion)
To your shack, the swap yard and the brothel's back alley.

## Open questions
- How much platforming should the chapter have? Act 1's back routes had hazards that taught one at a time.
