## Purpose

## When it's visited
Afternoons to nights. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Below ground, in a sunken compartment of something big: you go down a ramp into it. Low light over the tables, faces in shadow, the rattle of the game. Debts are chalked on a wall.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen.
- The ramp down.

- The house's cage, where stakes are held.
- The debt wall.

## Key props (suggestion)
