# Area brief: the tally house

Chapter 2. Built 2026-10-07 (`src/data/areas/borderland/tallyHouse.ts`, `story/borderland/tally-house.ink`; dev `?area=tally-house`). Inside a hull (`walls: 'hull'`). The walkway's door leads to the compound. The tallyman, the queue and the watchers come with the cast.

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Where finds are weighed and taxed each evening: this chapter's version of Act 1's evening ceremony. **(doc)** Tribute night shows how the town runs. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Every evening, when everyone queues with the day's haul. **(suggestion)** Tribute night happens in the first part of the chapter. **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A long, low hall built into the hull of something enormous: ribbed metal walls curving overhead. It's lit amber by lamps that hang above the scales. The queue snakes along a rail. It's quiet the way Act 1's rites are quiet: everyone is watching what everyone else brought.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
A little wider than one screen.
- The queue along the rail on the left.
- The tallyman's counter in the middle, with the great scale.
- The tribute pile behind a grille on the right.
- A raised walkway above, where the boss's people watch.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the great scale, built from parts nobody understands: a polished dish on a beam, with counterweights made of finds.
- A slate where each name and haul is marked.
- The grille cage with the clan's share.

## People
- The tallyman. **(doc)**
- The queue of scavengers.
- The boss's people on the walkway: the watcher on tribute night. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Join the queue, which hands in your haul and plays the weighing. You can also talk to the tallyman, read the slate, and talk to people in the queue.

## Exits (suggestion)
To the settlement: the swap yard and the compound.

## Open questions
- Is tribute a share of what you find, or a fixed amount you have to reach? (Under a fixed amount, you could fall short.)
- What happens to someone who comes up short?
