# Area brief: the lower roots

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/lowerRoots.ts`, `story/lower-roots.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The deepest, most crowded part of the roots, home to many of the tree's poorest, the girl among
them; glowing fungi. Nobody calls it rot, because nobody believes the tree is rotting. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Night 4: the rot, sneaking down to the girl's home. The roots ending. **(doc)** Stealth has to be
considered here. **(designer)** Built as a place for now; stealth comes later. **(designer)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Dark, packed and poor: patched plank shacks stacked two storeys high between huge roots, washing
lines, ladders, burrows dug into the walls above. Green fungus light everywhere (eerie; secretly a
sign of the rot), a shared fire under a communal pot, a few amber windows. Soft, damp patches of
wood with fungi thickest round them. Almost no tech.

## Size and layout
Larger than one screen **(designer)**: 1120×400, the camera follows you. **(suggestion)**
- A crowded, lit street along the bottom: shacks, the shared pot, people.
- A dark high route above: a root shelf, a rope bridge, two root walkways with a gap to jump. You
  reach it by jumping up shack roofs. Both routes lead to the far, deepest end.
- The girl's home at the far right **(designer)**, behind a great root, through a rag curtain.

For Night 4 (later): the street is lit and watched, the high route dark but with gaps and
drops; washing lines and shacks give cover. **(suggestion)**

## Key props (suggestion)
Shacks, great root pillars, the rope bridge, washing lines, ladders, burrows, bracket fungi,
soft patches, the communal pot. The girl's home: a hammock, a fungus lamp, her finds on strings
and in a heap, potted fungi, clothes on a line, lines scratched into the wall like a map only she
can read (a hint of the back routes), and her wall art in glowing fungus ink.

## People
- The girl: edgy, pierced and tattooed, slightly dismissive but flirty. **(designer)** Choppy,
  messy dark hair (no shaved side **(designer)**) with the glowing green strand **(doc)**, rings in
  her ear, brow and lip, fungus-ink tattoos, arms crossed. **(suggestion)** At home at all times
  for now.
- A crowd in the street morning to evening, hardly anyone at night; one talkable local.
  Background characters get proper looks when Act 1 is polished. **(designer)**
