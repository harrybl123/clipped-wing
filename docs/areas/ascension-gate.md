## When it's visited
Day 1 evening: a celebrated master is given to the tube (you notice their spiral). Day 7 night: the
tube ending, where you jump in front of everyone and Act 1 ends as you rise. **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The tube rises through the middle into the Canopy's glare, brightest of anywhere. Stairs climb
both sides to a dais before the gate: a riveted metal opening in the tube's wall with strips of
cyan light, the most machine-like thing in the tree, flanked by two great carved wings (the finest
carving; a nod to the title). Braziers burn on the dais; banners with the tube's symbol.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
