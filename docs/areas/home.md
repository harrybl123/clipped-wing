# Area brief: your home

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/home.ts`). Recorded here as a worked example of a brief.

## Purpose
Where you live with Mum. Dinner, the shrinking food store, and decoding messages in secret.

## When it's visited
Every evening; Nights 2 and 3.

## Mood and light
Poor but cosy and homely, like the Weasleys' Burrow crossed with nature and scavenged tech.
Mostly natural wood; a few scavenged gifts from the tube, which glow cyan. Warm amber from the
hearth and a candle; a green fungus lamp; green rot-light leaking through a crack in the bark.

## Size and layout
One screen, two levels in a hollowed-out root. Ground floor shared with Mum: door, kitchen corner,
hearth, table, food store with a living nook underneath. The loft is yours: bed, carving bench,
a shelf of your finished red birds, a loose board hiding place. Root steps lead up.

## Key props
Hearth with a scavenged metal chimney pipe; pulley basket between floors; drip channel and bucket;
patched walls; the crack on the rot side. Signature: your shelf of red birds.

## People
Mum (placeholder patterned shawl; she has no signature detail in the design doc yet).

## Interactions
Mum (talk), kitchen, hearth, food store (jars shrink with the `food_store` ink variable), seat,
bed, carving bench, hiding spot. All placeholder text in `story/home.ink`.

## Exits
Door → the offering floor (door menu also offers the map).

## Open questions
- Mum's signature detail.
- What sleeping, carving and the hiding spot actually do in play.
