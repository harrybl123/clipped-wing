# Area brief: the seed grove

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/seedGrove.ts`, `story/seed-grove.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Where healthy seeds grow. **(doc)** Message 4 asks for "new life that can't fly": a seed.
Night 5: the heist, if the archivist isn't your ally (it fails, and you take a rotten roots seed
without realising). **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The only gardens in the tree, out along a branch, flooded with the Canopy's white light through an
opening, with pollen drifting in it and vines trailing from the edge. Raised beds of seedlings,
saplings in tubs, glass cloches over precious seedlings, a vine-covered trellis, irrigation from
scavenged pipes. At the far end, a locked vault of glass jars whose seeds glow a warm amber
(living, healthy; the eerie green glow stays with the fungi of the roots).

## Size and layout (suggestion)
640×270. In over a rope bridge across a drop from the upper halls; beds and saplings on the floor;
steps up to a terrace with the trellis; the vault at the end. A dead end, which suits a heist.

## People (suggestion)
A gardener by day; a guard walking the grove at night (the beds and the terrace give cover).

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to the gardener; look at a cloche, the saplings and the vault. Placeholder text only.

## Exits
Left, over the bridge → the upper halls.

## Open questions
- How the heist fails, and where the rotten roots seed comes from.
- Does the healthy seed (the ally route) come from here too, handed over by the archivist?
