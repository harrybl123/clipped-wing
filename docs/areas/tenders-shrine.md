# Area brief: the Tender's shrine

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/tendersShrine.ts`, `story/tenders-shrine.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Where the Tender works and meets the elders. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Day 6 afternoon: tailing the Tender to a meeting with the preacher and elders, overheard. Night 6:
the interrogation, where every lie has to hold. **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Official and a little grand for the roots: carved pillars, high-backed chairs, birds carved along
the beam (bare wood: only real birds, the offerings, are red), amber lamplight and a chandelier.
A blessing enshrined in the wall behind the council, glowing like the tube: authority borrowed
from above. More tech than the homes, because blessings pass through here.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen. Left: the Tender's desk, tally boards (pictographs and notches, since nobody can read),
a shelf. Middle: a single stool alone under a low lamp, facing the council (where you'll be
questioned), and a grate in the floor (the back routes are beneath: the listening spot). Right:
a dais two steps up with four elders' high chairs under the enshrined blessing.

## People (suggestion)
- The Tender, at the desk in the afternoon (the rites on the offering floor are morning and
  evening now).
- Four elders and the preacher, on the dais in the afternoon for now. Placeholder looks from the
  villager generator: grey hair, long robes with an amber band and a pale stole; the preacher tall
  and hooded with a band of the tube's cyan. Proper looks when Act 1 is polished. **(designer)**
- Empty at night.

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to the Tender, an elder and the preacher; look at the tallies, the stool and the blessing.
Placeholder text only.

## Exits
Left arch → the offering floor. The floor grate → the back routes. Right arch on the dais → the
trunk climb (not built yet). **(suggestion)**
