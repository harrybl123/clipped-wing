# Area brief: the roots market

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/rootsMarket.ts`, `story/roots-market.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Rations, gossip, trading. **(doc)** Someone grumbles that portions are smaller. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Days 1, 2, 4 (the fight) and 5. **(doc)**

## Mood and light
- A proper working-class, energetic market, with some colour against the wooden backdrop. **(designer)**
- A small dripping of tech, since these are the lower roots. **(designer)**
- Colour from striped awnings, bolts and hanging lengths of cloth, and bunting in cyan, amber,
  green and pale (the palette, never red). Amber lanterns, a brazier, green fungus jars, and cyan
  from the scavenged tech. **(suggestion)**
- Busy morning and afternoon; quieter in the evening (people are at the ceremony); empty at night.
  **(suggestion)**

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen, two levels, at the bottom of a hollow with homes carved into the walls above (lit
windows, baskets hauled up on ropes). Ground floor: food stall with a brazier, fungus stall, the
ration hatch with a queue, cloth stall. Steps up to a balcony: curiosities and tools.

## Key props (suggestion)
Striped stalls, pictograph signs (no writing: roots-dwellers can't read), cook pot and produce,
fungus jars, cloth, the ration hatch, sacks and crates, bunting, lanterns. Tech: a cracked
glowing screen and odd bits on the curiosities stall; a lantern made from a light cell.

## People (suggestion)
Food, fungus, cloth, curiosities and tools sellers; a ration queue including the grumbler;
villagers wandering and browsing (new: walk cycles).

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to the food, fungus, cloth and curiosities sellers and the grumbler; look at the ration
hatch. All placeholder text.

## Exits
Left → the offering floor. Right → your friend's home (not built yet).
