## Purpose
Wealth and warmth; meeting the archivist. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Day 5: the escort brings you up for your first sight of the upper branches' wealth; the archivist
asks to meet you (big choice 2: truth or pander). **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The richest, brightest place so far. The Canopy's white light falls through skylights (the first
place it reaches), dust drifting in it; a chandelier; light cells in brass sconces. The most tech
in the tree: a fountain lifted by a turning gear. A feast table laden while the roots go hungry.
Writing, which belongs to high society: a brass plaque by the door, shelves of books.

Height is skill, so the carving is spectacular **(designer)**: a masterwork centrepiece (a flock of
carved birds spiralling up a braided trunk to a great bird with its wings spread, on a carved
plinth, lit by a skylight), ornate carved columns, a frieze of vines and birds along the walls,
all bare polished wood.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
720×270. The way in from the middle tiers with the plaque; the feast; the masterwork; the fountain;
stairs up to a library on a mezzanine (bookcases, a reading desk) where the archivist waits; a
lounge beneath it.
