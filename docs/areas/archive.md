# Area brief: the archive

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/archive.ts`, `story/archive.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The Ascension records. **(doc)** Night 5: with the archivist as an ally, a search puzzle in the
records (and a healthy seed); without, the heist, stealth that fails (and a rotten seed from the
roots). **(doc)** The only unguarded way in is the trunk climb's back hatch. **(designer)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Quiet, dusty and lamplit, with the Canopy's light through a round window and dust drifting in it.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
640×270. A gallery runs along a wall of records: a plaque for each of the Ascended, each with
writing (unreadable to you) and the maker's carved mark (a picture anyone can recognise); the
newest plaque is still pale from the chisel. Beneath the gallery: record drawers, reading desks,
the archivist's desk. By the stairs: the index engine, a scavenged cabinet of drawers worked by a
crank and a great gear, with a glowing lens over the drawer you want (a possible basis for the
search puzzle). The back hatch in the floor; the far door on the gallery.

## People (suggestion)
The archivist at their desk in the morning (in the upper halls in the afternoon and evening);
scribes by day; a watchman walking the floor at night (for the heist).

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to the archivist; look at the index engine, the records and the newest record; search the
drawers. Placeholder text only.

## Exits
The floor hatch → the trunk climb. The gallery's far door → the Ascension gate (not built yet).

## Open questions
- The Night 5 search puzzle: is it about maker's marks, the index engine, or something else?
- What the heist's watchman does when they see you.
