# Area brief: the offering floor — DRAFT for review

Drafted by Claude from the design doc. Lines marked **(doc)** come from the design; everything
marked **(suggestion)** is a proposal for you to keep, change or cut.

## Purpose
- The base of the tube: offerings go up at the **morning rite**, blessings and rejections come
  down at the **evening ceremony**. **(doc)**
- The roots are a tight hub around it; you return here every morning and evening. **(doc)**
- The Tender collects offerings here and announces blessings. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
- Every morning and evening. **(doc)**
- Day 2 evening: your bird is the first rejection in living memory. Day 6 evening: your bird comes
  back bitten, and the crowd recoils. Day 7 morning: you send your bird in a cage. **(doc)**

## Mood and light
- The one bright, public place in the roots: the tube's cold cyan light pours down the middle and
  everyone faces it. Ceremonial, crowded, a holiness that's slightly unnerving. **(suggestion)**
- A little more wealth than your home: one or two amber lamps near the Tender's stand. Green fungi
  only at the dim edges. **(suggestion)**
- Technology: a few scavenged fittings around the basin, glowing faintly cyan. **(suggestion)**

## Size and layout (suggestion)
One screen, symmetrical around the tube:

```
        ░░ tube comes down through the ceiling ░░
  ledges │          ║ tube ║          │ ledges
  (crowd)│   lamp   ║      ║  Tender's│ (crowd)
  ═══════╡          ║      ║  stand   ╞═══════
 exit ←  │  offering  ╔════════╗        │  → exit
 (home)  │  rack     ═╝ basin  ╚═ steps │  (market)
  ═══════╧════════════════════════════════════════
```

- The tube ends in a carved **basin** on a raised, round platform with steps. Nobody else stands on it.
- The **Tender's stand** beside the basin, under an amber lamp.
- An **offering rack** where the day's carvings wait before the rite.
- Tiered **root ledges** either side where the crowd stands (platforms you can jump up to).

## Key props (suggestion)
Basin, steps, Tender's stand with an amber lamp, offering rack, blessing baskets for what comes
down, worn floor where people kneel. Signature: the tube meeting the basin.

## People
- **The Tender** **(doc)**: official who collects offerings and announces blessings; face of the
  rules and suspicion; thinks they understand your rejection, and is wrong. Signature detail: an
  **amber crest of office**. **(doc)**
- A few background villagers, placeholder, with no speaking role beyond a line each. **(suggestion)**

## Exits (suggestion)
- Left → your home (your home is on the edge of the roots **(doc)**).

- The door menu offers the map for everywhere else (Tender's shrine, friend's home, back routes).

## Open questions
1. What do other people's offerings look like? Only yours are red birds.
2. How do blessings come down: baskets on ropes, drifting down the tube, placed by the Tender?
3. Is the basin forbidden ground (the player can't step onto it)?
4. How big a crowd, and does it change between morning and evening?
5. The Tender's look beyond the amber crest: robes, age, posture?
