# Area brief: the offering floor

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/offeringFloor.ts`, `story/offering-floor.ink`).
**(doc)** = from the design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's
proposal, not yet confirmed.

## Purpose
- The base of the tube: offerings go up at the **morning rite**, blessings and rejections come
  down at the **evening ceremony**. **(doc)**
- The roots are a tight hub around it; you return here every morning and evening. **(doc)**
- The Tender collects offerings and announces blessings. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
- Every morning and evening. **(doc)**
- Day 2 evening: the first rejection in living memory. Day 6 evening: your bird comes back bitten
  and the crowd recoils. Day 7 morning: you send your bird in a cage. **(doc)**

## Mood and light
- The one bright, public place in the roots; the tube's cold cyan light pours down the middle.
- A crowd for the ceremonies (morning and evening); otherwise not too busy. **(designer)**
  Afternoon: a couple of people passing through; night: empty. **(suggestion)**
- The Tender's amber lamp; green fungi at the dim edges; banners with the tube's symbol
  (roots-dwellers can't read, so no writing). **(suggestion)**

## Size and layout
- A platform in the middle that rises like a staircase up to the blue beam. **(designer)**
- One screen, symmetrical: stairs up both sides to a landing; the tube comes down through a carved
  ring in the ceiling into a basin on the landing. Doorway arches at each side. **(suggestion)**
- The player walks up the stairs without jumping (low ledges are stepped up automatically).

## Key props
Tube and basin, ceiling ring, the Tender's hanging lamp, two banners, blessing baskets and kneeling
mats at the foot of the stairs, fungi. **(suggestion)**

## People
- **The Tender**: stands on the landing under a lamp, watching things come in and out of the tube.
  **(designer)** Wizardy, wise and old **(designer)**: tall and stooped, long robe, hood with the
  **amber crest of office** **(doc)**, long white beard, a staff. Here morning, afternoon and
  evening; gone at night. **(suggestion)** Thinks they understand your rejection, and is wrong. **(doc)**
- The crowd: eight varied villagers, background only. One grumbler in the afternoon (smaller
  portions **(doc)**). **(suggestion)**

## Interactions
The Tender (talk; placeholder uses the three dialogue styles as choices), the tube (look), the
baskets (look), the grumbler (talk). All placeholder text.

## Exits
Left → your home. Right → the roots market (not built yet). The door menu offers the map.
