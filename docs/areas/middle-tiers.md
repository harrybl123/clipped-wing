# Area brief: the middle tiers

Built 2026-09-30 (`src/data/areas/middleTiers.ts`, `story/middle-tiers.ink`).
**(doc)** = design doc; **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Passing through: better homes, more light. **(doc)** The official way up through the trunk: a
grand stair-street with a guard post at its foot, where roots-dwellers are turned back unless
they've been sent for, which is why the trunk climb is the unguarded back way. Height is skill,
so master crafters work here. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Day 5: the escort takes you up (your first sight of the upper branches' wealth). Day 7: the
chase. **(doc)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Warm amber everywhere: lit windows, glass lanterns, neat panelled house fronts with carved trim and
window boxes of greenery, hanging planters, banners. The tube glows behind the first landing.
More tech than below: a scavenged gear clock, cell lanterns, a counterweight lift.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
960×400, the camera follows you. Two walkable flights of stairs climb left to right. The foot:
the way in from the trunk climb, the guard post, houses, a gallery above. The first landing: a
master carver's workshop (fine figures on shelves: it should sting a little for a carver from
the roots), a railed view of the tube, homes below and beside. The top landing: the way on to the
upper halls, a fine house. The lift runs up the right-hand side, from the floor to the top landing,
"for those who can pay".

## People (suggestion)

residents strolling (new placeholder looks, better dressed).
