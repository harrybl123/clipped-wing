# Area brief: the low district

Chapter 3. Not built yet. Names are placeholders (designer).

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
Cramped and full of the symbol. **(doc)** Where the unrest is visible, and where your hand takes you to see what's really going on: the people know who you are, so they're cautious, but they want to tell you. **(designer)** If the power stays with high society, it's the low district that rises. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Trips below with your hand; the riot, if it's here. **(designer, doc, suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Under a ceiling of cables slung between stacked homes. Patched, lived in: washing lines, pots of plants, cooking smells. Less power, so more amber from lamps and fires than cyan. The symbol chalked everywhere. Where the power's cut, no light at all.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Two screens wide, outdoors, with homes stacked up both sides, walkways between them, and a way down to the hideout.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the cables overhead, and the symbol chalked on the walls.
- Stacked homes, stalls, a dead screen.

## People
The painter (a kid who chalks the symbol). **(doc)** People who know who you are. **(designer)** The district's firebrand, if it rises. **(doc)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to people.

## Exits (suggestion)
The lifts; the promenade; the hideout.
