# Area brief: the promenade

Chapter 3. Not built yet. Names are placeholders (designer).

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The mid-city market. **(doc)** Where the city meets: the middle of it, between the citadel and the low district. If you give the power to the people, it's the promenade that goes dark and rises, led by the reformer. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Trips below with your hand; the riot, if it's here. **(doc, suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The first outdoor area in the city, so it sets the street look: shopfronts under signs of cyan and amber light, screens over the street, steam from food stalls, crowds, rain at night. The citadel high above.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Two screens wide, outdoors: stalls and shopfronts along the street, a raised walkway above, a great screen over the square.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the great screen over the square.
- Stalls, signs, steam.

## People
Traders; shoppers; the city. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Talk to people.

## Exits (suggestion)
The lifts; the low district; the gate.
