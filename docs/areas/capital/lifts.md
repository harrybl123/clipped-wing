## Purpose
Glass lifts between levels, where messages change hands. **(doc)** The way between the citadel and the city.

## When it's visited
Whenever you go down. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
A tall area up the face of the city: glass lift cages riding up and down, the city's lights all round, the screens on the towers. Wind and rain.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Several screens high, like the trunk climb: stations at each level (the citadel at the top, the promenade, the data centre, the low district at the bottom), with lifts between them (the trunk climb's moving platforms).

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the glass lifts riding up the city's face.
- The operator's booth at the bottom station.

## People
The lift operator: sees who rides where, and when. **(doc)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
Ride the lifts; talk to the operator.

## Exits (suggestion)
The audience hall (top); the promenade, the data centre, the low district.
