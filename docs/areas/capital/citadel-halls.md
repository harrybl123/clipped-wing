# Area brief: the citadel's halls

Chapter 3. Not built yet. Names are placeholders (designer).

**(doc)** = chapter design, **(designer)** = decided in conversation, **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal.

## Purpose
The corridors that join the citadel's rooms: you walk between them (designer: corridors in the citadel). Where you pass people on the way, and overhear things. **(suggestion)**

## When it's visited
Every day, going from room to room. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
Long marble halls, gold inlay in the floor, blue light in the seams of the walls and up the columns. Tall windows over the city. Screens at the corners. Guards at the doors, servants passing, courtiers talking low.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
Two floors joined by a grand stair, about three screens wide.
- **Below, the state floor:** the doors to the audience hall (great double doors), the council chamber, the banquet gallery and the glasshouse.
- **Above, the private floor:** your quarters, your father's study, the diplomat's office and the guest wing.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the grand stair, marble and gold, with the light running up its rail.
- Tall windows over the city; screens; statues of rulers before your father.

## People
Guards, servants, courtiers; whoever's passing between rooms. **(suggestion)**

## Interactions (suggestion)
The doors; talk to people passing.

## Exits (suggestion)
Every citadel room except the sealed wing (through the study).
