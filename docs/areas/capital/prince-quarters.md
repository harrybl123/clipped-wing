## Purpose
Your private rooms, with a balcony over the city. **(doc)** Where each day starts and ends, and where you're alone with what you've learned. The tree is on the horizon. **(doc)**

## When it's visited
Every morning and night. **(suggestion)** The night before the broadcast. **(suggestion)**

## Mood and light (suggestion)
The first area, so it sets the look: white marble, gold inlay, a soft blue glow in the seams of the walls. Calm and expensive, too quiet. Through the balcony doors, the city below: cyan and amber lights, the cables, the low district in shadow at the bottom. Far off, the tree.

## Size and layout (suggestion)
A little wider than one screen. The bedroom on the left, a sitting room, then the balcony on the right, open to the sky, where you can stand at the rail and look out.

## Key props (suggestion)
- **Signature:** the balcony rail, with the whole city below and the tree on the horizon.
- A bed, a wall screen (the news, and later your father's face), a gold-framed mirror, a wardrobe with the plain clothes you wear below.
