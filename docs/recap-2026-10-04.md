# Story session recap (2026-10-04)

What we settled today, in short. Full detail lives in [ideas.md](ideas.md).

## The world

- Humans created AI; survival meant integrating with it. Those who did are **the integrated**, running on biocomputing (cell biology, far less energy).
- The game follows the **unintegrated**: humans who refused, living in the shadow of beings they can never compete with.
- Societies like the tree get resources because their imperfect, natural work has value to the integrated. **The player must not learn this** outside the tree's own story.

## The structure

- After Act 1, the game switches characters so the tree's mystery stays intact.
- A political and societal RPG across four leads who eventually meet.

## The four leads (final)

1. **The carver**: lives among the high scavengers in the tree's canopy. Grounded was an old master who ascended and ranks high among them. He made the carver his tool. Conflict: control the tree's society, or reveal the truth without destabilising it. Played through message manipulation and espionage inside the tree. Act 1's ending changes how much power your messages carry.
2. **The scavenger**: a sister feeding her little brother on the dangerous, mafia-run ground. Her secret is a dog in a world where dogs are nearly extinct.
3. **The hitman**: a semi-integrated freelancer, not sent by the integrated. Arrives once the player understands how the world works.
4. **The prince**: an heir in one of the last human cities, with a diplomat at his side, living through a plot to overthrow the ruling party.

## The scavenger's chapter (fully designed)

- **Setting:** a scavenger town at the tree's borderland. Tax and food are paid in finds. Locations: her shack, the tally house, the swap yard, the bar, the gambling den, the brothel, the boss's compound, the scrap stacks, the picked fields, the sunk town, the dog's hollow, the rival line, the deep ground and the tree's edge.
- **Conflict:** the rival clan is raiding her clan's turf, and raids keep happening wherever she has been. The dog's finds got her brought into the boss's circle, and now she's suspected as the rat. Many people look guilty, but the real rat is the boss, using her as a scapegoat.
- **The boss's motive:** his daughter is held by the rival clan, and every secret he passes keeps her alive. Befriend the rivals and you find out, and neither side is right.
- **Cast:** the boss, the watcher (quiet, intimidating, secretly fair, the one you must convince), the enforcer, the boss's son; her brother, her friend, the old scavenger, the gambler, the kid lookout, the girl who vanished; the tallyman, barkeep, madam and fence.
- **Big decisions:**
  1. Move your brother into the boss's compound? Yes: he's brainwashed and gives away the dog. No: he becomes your witness, but gets caught and hurt. Either way you leave without him.
  2. Turn in a rival scout to the watcher, or let them go and befriend the rival clan.
  3. Before you leave: save the boss's daughter, end the conflict (costs you the dog's secret; she keeps the dog), or let the clans burn.
- **Medium decisions:** what to do with the dog (always ends with keeping it); your friend's secret; the gambler's debt (helping earns a weapon); the rival drop the dog finds (a clue, never the full answer).
