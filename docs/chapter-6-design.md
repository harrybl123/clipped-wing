# Clipped Wing — Chapter 6: The Scavenger and the Hunter

Oct 4, 2026 · Harry Breeze-Lamb

Design for the sixth playable chapter, where the game returns to the scavenger. The wider cast and world notes live in [ideas.md](ideas.md). Earlier chapters: [chapter-2-design.md](chapter-2-design.md), [chapter-3-design.md](chapter-3-design.md), [chapter-4-design.md](chapter-4-design.md), [chapter-5-design.md](chapter-5-design.md).

## Pitch

The scavenger, exiled at the end of chapter 2, is out in the forest near the tree with her dog. The canopy's hunter has been tracking her since he found dog prints at the end of chapter 4. They end up in a standoff, then strike a business deal: he's hunting a great beast that could feed the canopy for weeks, and he can't find it without her dog's nose. On the hunt they slowly build a relationship, he tells her about the revolution, she finds her brother, and by the end she decides to go up the tree.

## What she knows

- She already knows what happens to the tree's people.
- What's new to her is the revolution. She learns about it from the hunter, thinks it's awful at first, and is slowly persuaded.

## Carried over

- **She always has the dog.** Chapter 2 always ends with her keeping it.
- **The rival hunters know about the dog** if she ended the conflict in chapter 2 (she revealed it to both clans to seal the truce). Whatever happened, the dog is no longer a secret on the ground.
- **Grounded's fate stays unknown.** If the player shot Grounded at the end of chapter 5, this chapter doesn't reveal what happened.

## Chapter shape

**Structure (Harry, 2026-10-04):** a moveable area like most chapters, not a set-piece chapter. Days in the forest around her camp and the family's homestead, tracking the beast as the trail develops, broken up by set pieces for the big beats: the standoff, the rescue, the beast fight and the climb.

1. **The standoff:** the hunter tracks her and the dog down in the forest.
2. **The deal:** the dog's help with the great beast, in exchange for a share of the kill and safe passage.
3. **The road:** the hunt keeps them busy, and the relationship builds. Around the fires he tells her about the revolution.
4. **The brother:** the hunter stops to trade with the quartermaster's family, whom he knows. The dog goes wild in a way it only ever does for one person, so she knows straight away: her brother is there, where the hitman left him. He tells her about the man who saved him, and why. What he says depends on what he saw in chapter 5 (whether the hitman killed the boss or let him live).
5. **The rival hunters:** a ground clan hunting party from her old borderland is after the same beast, led by the boss's son.
6. **The beast:** a three-way boss fight with the scavenger, the hunter and the dog. The dog finds the weak spot, the hunter brings it down, and she finishes it.
7. **The way up:** at the tree's foot she watches the hitman's approach, and decides to go up. Her brother comes up to the canopy with her whichever way she climbs.

**Later:** the boy is the reason the hitman is freed in the canopy. The child who wasn't afraid of him vouches for him. This works after either chapter 5 ending, because the canopy holds the hitman either way: as a wounded prisoner if he fired, or under guard after the talk if he didn't.

## Locations

The forest and the ground around the tree.

- **Her camp:** a shelter at the forest's edge, where the chapter opens.
- **The deep forest:** old-world ruins swallowed by growth. The beast's territory.
- **The family's homestead:** the quartermaster's family, where the hitman left her brother.
- **The tribe's hunting camp:** where the rival hunters take the hunter. The rescue set piece.
- **The beast's lair:** the boss fight.
- **The tree's foot:** the roots, where both routes up begin.
- **The outer trunk:** the outside climb.

## Cast

- **The scavenger, the dog, and her brother.**
- **The hunter:** canopy-born, wiry, laughs at danger, and just wants everyone fed (from chapter 4).
- **The quartermaster's family:** her old father and her sister, wary but decent.
- **The boss's son:** leads the rival hunters. He was jealous of her rise in chapter 2, and now he's hunting the dog everyone knows about.
- **Her friend:** rides with the tribe, because she was secretly seeing one of them in chapter 2.

## Big decisions

Four this time.

### 1. The rival tribe

Agree to hunt with them or not. Either way, they take the hunter, who to them is a canopy raider on their turf.

### 2. The rescue

- **Sneak in:** get the hunter out quietly. The tribe can still help you later (for example, when the borderlands could unite).
- **Kill them:** a violent rescue. The tribe is gone, and can't help you out later.

### 3. The son's question

The boss's son asks her about the hitman.

- **Tell him the truth:** it hurts her later, because the hitman turns out to be an ally, and now the son is out for him.
- **Lie:** it stays buried.

### 4. The way up

- **Through the tree:** the next tree chapter opens with her learning the ways of its people.
- **Race up the outside:** she helps the carver save Grounded. The first time those two leads meet.

## Medium decisions

- **Her friend** (links to chapter 2, your friend's secret): if she covered for her friend, the friend offers to help her get into the tribe's camp. If she reported her, the friend is hostile. Trust her offer or not.

## Open questions

- What "help the carver save Grounded" looks like, given the chapter 5 cliffhanger.
- How the hitman is freed, and what the boy says.
- Combat details for the beast fight.
- Names, including the beast.
