# Chapter 4 world: the canopy

The world for [Chapter 4](chapter-4-design.md) ([the plan](sequences/chapter-4/plan.md)), built the way the other chapters' were: the world first (area briefs, the map, then each area as data with placeholder art), then the cast, then the story day by day.

Labels: **(doc)** comes from the chapter design, **(designer)** was decided in conversation, and **(suggestion)** is Claude's proposal, kept until you confirm or change it. Names are placeholders for now.

## The idea

- **A treehouse civilisation in the canopy, where height means status** (doc). The people who've been receiving the tree's offerings all along, led by Grounded (doc).
- **They're scavengers really** (designer): they live off what the tree sends up. The offerings feed and furnish them before the rest goes on, down the lowering platform to the outside.
- **Furnished with the tree's own work** (designer: good suggestion): offerings that never went on line every rail, hang from every eave, prop every door. Act 1's work is everywhere you look: the roots' ornaments, the upper branches' masterwork, a thousand carved birds.
- **For the carver, the shock of daylight** (designer: scared and shocked by everything): Act 1 was dark wood, fungus glow and the tube's light, with the Canopy only ever a white glare overhead. Up here is sky, sun and wind.
- The lookout sees the capital's lights (doc), as the prince sees the tree from his balcony.

## Look

**Revised after the first look (designer, 2026-10-17): a village among the limbs, like the Ewoks' in Return of the Jedi.** Every area is built round the tree's giant limbs: ring platforms round them, round huts under tall thatched roofs, rope bridges, plank stairs; nothing floats; a calm, hazy background of limbs so what's near reads clearly. What follows was the first idea; the solarpunk touches (sails, turbines, lanterns, water) stay, as accents.

- **Solarpunk** (designer): futurism mixed with living in treehouses.
  - **Living wood and green** (suggestion): platforms grown from the branches, not nailed to them; walls of woven boughs and carved panels; gardens on every deck, vines, hanging planters, beehives. Green belongs to the tree (Chapter 2's rule), and up here it's everywhere.
  - **Clean, bright tech in the leaves** (suggestion): solar sails cut like leaves among the real ones, small wind turbines turning on the high branches, glass lanterns that charge by day, water caught on wide funnels and piped down to every house, pulley lifts, cable runs with baskets. Radios (the hunter's), the electric bow. Nothing dark, nothing smoking: a place built to last.
  - **The tubes** (doc): out of the top of the tree like an octopus's tentacles, the tube's cyan light inside them; from outside, a bundle of pipes rising out of the middle of the canopy (Chapter 2).
  - **The scavenged** (suggestion): the solarpunk is patched with what's sent up: carved panels from the tree's offerings set into walls, ornaments for door handles, a master's chair as a bench in the mess. Pretty, and a little wrong when you know where it came from.
- **Palette** (suggestion): leaf greens (the root green's shades) and warm wood; sunlight and sky (canopy white: the "unknowable top of the tree", known at last); amber for lamps, honey and warmth; the tube's cyan in the tentacles and in the tech's small lights.
- **Red stays reserved** (palette): birds and the protagonist only. **Your birds are the only red up here** (suggestion): every one you ever sent, on Grounded's shelves. Copper and wine stay reserved too.
- **Drawn in colour** (suggestion), like the scavenger and the prince: it's daylight. The carver too: a new colour sprite of you for the canopy (the red thread, the bandaged hand, then the metal fist), as the tree's rim-lit figure was for the dark.
- **Time of day** (suggestion):
  - morning: low sun through the leaves, dappled light moving on the decks, mist below
  - afternoon: bright and hazy, the solar sails turned to the sun
  - evening: gold, then the lanterns coming on
  - night: stars, the lanterns, the tentacles glowing cyan, the capital's lights on the horizon

## Height means status (suggestion)

The doc says height means status; who lives where:

- **The top:** Grounded's house, the highest and oldest (doc), with the lookout above it; the seed bed beside it. The people who run things.
- **The middle:** the great house (the sorting deck, the blessing workshop, the mess, most people's homes: doc), the tube mouth, the ascended's hall, the message loft. Canopy-born families and the ascended.
- **The edges and the bottom:** the lowering platform, out on a long branch over the drop; the homes of people who climbed up from the ground (the quartermaster started there: doc).
- **The irony** (suggestion): in the tree the ascended were the very top; up here they're workers, making blessings to order (doc: the master).

## The map (suggestion)

A fourth world on the map: the treetop side-on, a cutaway like the tree's. The tentacles in the middle, the great house round them, walkways out to the treehouses on the branches, Grounded's house at the top, the lowering platform's cable down off one side to the ground. Places shown as lit windows, as on the tree's map. The way down to the inside (the hatches) and the way down to the ground (the lowering platform) go off the edge of the map, to the tree's map and the borderland's.

## The people

### Grounded (designer)

- **A Nathan Drake / Joel (*The Last of Us*) type** (designer; a type, not a likeness): rugged, weathered, a working man.
- **Passionate, slightly aggressive** (designer). Charismatic and theatrical (doc).
- **An ascended master, angry he was lied to for so long** (designer), **which is why you can relate to each other** (designer). He talks to you like an old friend because he's been reading your birds for weeks (doc).
- He preaches freedom while pulling your strings, and can't see the contradiction (doc).
- (Suggestion) **He went up young**: masters go up at the end of a life of work (the prologue), and he was the youngest the Tree ever chose, so the lie took more of his life than anyone's. Middle-aged now. **A carver** (suggestion), which is how he read the same flaw in every one of your birds.
- (Suggestion) **Looks:** stubble going grey, hair pushed back, sleeves rolled, a canopy harness over a worn shirt, a carving knife at his belt he still uses. His spiral (every master cuts it into their last piece) tattooed on his forearm.

### The colony (doc, with suggested looks)

- **The master** (doc; designer: finds you at the tube mouth): Day 1's master, old and calm (Act 1's sprite to work from), now in a workshop apron with wood dust in her hair and a bottle within reach. Bitter, mocks the faith she embodied; gentle with you on the first morning (designer). Hides her spiral in every blessing (doc). The delivery is hers (agreed).
- **The quartermaster** (doc): ground-born, clawed her way up; ruthless and funny. (Suggestion) Broad, a scarf, a ring of keys and a tally slate, sleeves always rolled.
- **The doubter** (doc): a former roots-dweller with a good life up here; the Tender's man in the canopy (agreed). (Suggestion) Too clean, too pleased with his garden; Act 1's roots in his face and his manners.
- **The hatch-keeper** (doc): canopy-born, never been inside, obsessed with the tree like a fairytale, reckless. (Suggestion) Young, ropes and a harness, goggles up, a book of drawings of the inside made from what people have told them.
- **The messenger** (doc): cool, polished, never stays a night, a hint of the capital. (Suggestion) A capital coat (Chapter 3's cut) under a traveller's cloak.
- **The hunter** (designer): Sevro au Barca (*Red Rising*) as a type: small, wiry, feral, a fur mantle over his shoulders (designer, 2026-10-19: no pelt over his head, too close to his inspiration), foul-mouthed, fiercely loyal; the electric bow. (Suggestion) A radio on his chest.
- **The child who bit your bird** (designer): small, hungry, mortified.
- **The ascended** (doc): the old masters, alive. (Suggestion) Older people at work, each with their spiral somewhere.
- **Canopy-born folk** (suggestion): light layered clothes in greens and creams, harnesses and clips (everyone's clipped on near a drop), sun hats, goggles, gardening gloves.
- **The quartermaster's family** (doc, Chapter 5): on the ground, wary, fed by her hoard.

## What the world has to show (from the plan)

- **Your arrival:** the sky for the first time (both routes).
- **The truth in things:** offerings arriving up the tentacles, sorted on the deck, some kept, the rest crated and lowered away; blessings made by hand in the workshop.
- **The town's mood by the seed** (designer): hopeful or grim, in what people say and do.
- **Battle 1 and battle 2** happen here (Jack's war table, the canopy as its board).
- **The hunger** (the world's economics) here too: the mess's portions.
- **Your message** made in the loft.

## New things to build (suggestion)

- **The canopy's look:** a backdrop of leaves, branches and sky, layered (the far canopy, the near boughs), for the time of day; light dappled through the leaves; living-wood platforms and woven walls (a new wall style); solar sails, turbines, lanterns, water funnels, cable baskets, bridges that sway.
- **A fourth map** (`WORLDS`), and the carver drawn in colour (with the metal fist from Day 2).
- **Sounds:** wind in the leaves, creaking boughs, birdsong (real birds, which the carver has never heard), the turbines, the tentacles' hum.
