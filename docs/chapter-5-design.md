# Clipped Wing — Chapter 5: The Hitman

Oct 4, 2026 · Harry Breeze-Lamb

Design for the fifth playable chapter, the hitman's journey to the tree. The wider cast and world notes live in [ideas.md](ideas.md). Earlier chapters: [chapter-2-design.md](chapter-2-design.md), [chapter-3-design.md](chapter-3-design.md), [chapter-4-design.md](chapter-4-design.md).

## Pitch

A half-integrated freelancer takes a job with a ridiculous price: kill Grounded, the leader of the revolution. On the road to the tree he slowly learns about the revolution, and becomes highly interested in it. By the time he has Grounded at gunpoint, he has enough intrigue to ask questions instead of just pulling the trigger.

- **This is where the player really begins to feel the effects of their decisions.** Almost every stop on the road is shaped by an earlier chapter.
- **Where the story is heading:** the four leads meet up and orchestrate a revolution.

## The hitman

- **Overpowered, but rejected by everyone for what he is.** He can win any fight, but nobody will let him in. He's too integrated for humans, and too human for the integrated.
- **The inversion:** every other lead struggles to survive and has to win people over. For him, power is never the obstacle; trust is. The haven tolerates him only for money, the managed places bar their doors, and the clans fear him.
- **He doesn't know about the revolution at the start.** He learns it piece by piece on the road.

## The mystery: who hired him

- **The client is the prince's father,** which links the hitman to the capital. It's kept hidden for a later twist.
- **First clue:** the contract is anonymous, but carries a hint of the capital's polish, and the price is ridiculous, so someone powerful wants this done.

## Shape: a string of set pieces

Unlike the other chapters, this isn't a week in one place. It's a journey through a string of set pieces, most of them shaped by earlier choices. Keep the presentation simple, even where the links are complex.

1. **The outlaw haven (fixed):** a cyberpunk refuge for outlaws, where he lives. The job comes to his flat.
2. **The convoy (chapter 4, the Tender)** (re-linked 2026-10-17: chapter 4's second big decision is now killing or sparing the Tender, the capital's agent in the tree; suggestion, to settle with what happens to him if spared):
   - *The Tender killed:* the capital's men escorting someone to the tree, to be their new eyes.
   - *The Tender spared:* a courier carrying the king's orders to the Tender, who now answers to the carver.
   - (Earlier draft, dropped with chapter 4's diversion and cell: a convoy of the tree's goods, starving scavengers, an empty cache, a runner from the lower roots.)
3. **Another managed place (chapter 3, the envoy's plea):** it's the envoy's home.
   - *If the prince granted the plea and his father quietly undid it:* a place promised relief by the heir and then had it taken back. Bitter towards the capital, the symbol on the walls.
   - *If the prince refused:* worn down and resigned, sending everything with no fight left. He sees the human cost of the system.
4. **The borderland (chapter 2, power before you leave):** he stops to set up a base before the tree and gets drawn into whatever conflict the scavenger left behind. The boss is the one holding the scavenger's little brother, whatever happened in chapter 2: exposed, but clinging on.
   - *Who's using the boy:* if she kept it raging, the boss at war; if she ended it, the boss under the truce; if she saved the girl, both clans are using the boy as bait to draw her back.
   - *The boy himself:* if he was brainwashed in the compound, he doesn't want to be rescued. If he was caught and hurt, he's desperate to go.
   - The hitman works out the truth about the boy, hurt or manipulated, and wants to save him. The boy is the only person on the road who isn't afraid of him.
   - **He always takes the boy with him** (whichever way decision 2 goes), and leaves him with the quartermaster's family on the ground, near the forest, before the climb (whatever he chose with them). In chapter 6 the scavenger finds her brother there, and learns about the hitman and his motives from him.
5. **The messenger (chapter 3, the broadcast):** they share the road, and he learns there's a network. How the messenger talks about the diplomat depends on where he ended up:
   - *Father's script (the diplomat fled):* the network's open, hunted leader, with the capital in turmoil.
   - *The diplomat's draft (still at the prince's side):* the messenger lets slip that they have "a man in the citadel".
   - *The prince's own words (the prince is close to his father):* the network is worried the citadel's door is closing.
6. **The climb (fixed):** the tree isn't free yet. He climbs it and finds Grounded.

## The outlaw haven

The hitman's own world. Every other stop on the road is one of the set pieces above.

**Places**
- **His flat:** high up and sparse, where the job arrives.
- **The mod market:** where outlaws buy and sell parts, half human and half machine.
- **The bar:** where he drinks alone, because nobody sits near him.

**People**
- **The fixer:** brokers his jobs and asks no questions. She brings him this contract, and even she won't say who it's from.
- **The mechanic:** keeps his integrated parts working. The only person who touches him without flinching, and only because he pays.

## Cast on the road

- **The overseer:** the capital's man in charge of the managed town, who offers him money (big decision 1).
- **From earlier chapters:** the gambler (in the haven), the mob boss and the scavenger's little brother (the borderland), the messenger, the prince's exiled friend (if the prince told the guard captain), the quartermaster's family, and Grounded.

## Big decisions

### 1. The managed place

He passes through a town run by the capital. The man in charge offers him money to deal with some locals who are causing trouble. He doesn't know about the revolution yet, so to him it's just a desperate place and a symbol he doesn't understand.

- **Take the job:** he hurts the locals and gets paid.
- **Turn on the man in charge:** he kills him, and the locals are left to face whatever the capital does next.

The link to chapter 3 only sets how angry the town is: a crowd if the prince's promise was broken, a few desperate people if he refused them outright. The choice is the same either way.

### 2. The borderland

Kill the mob boss or let him live.

- **Kill him:** the boy is free, and there's a power vacuum. That leaves room for the scavenger to come down later and unite the borderlands as they learn the truth.
- **Let him live:** he trades the boy for the boss's life, and in the bargaining the boss tells him why he did it: his daughter. A father doing terrible things to protect his own, like the man who hired the hitman. The boss keeps the borderlands, so the scavenger's return is harder.

### 3. Grounded at gunpoint

Shoot him or let him live.

- **Shoot:** you hear a gunshot and the screen goes black, straight into the next chapter. The player later finds out it was actually the hitman who was shot.
- **Let him live:** the questions happen face to face, as near equals.

## Medium decisions

Each links to a medium choice from an earlier chapter.

- **The gambler (chapter 2, the gambler's debt):** he's fled to the outlaw haven. If the scavenger helped him, he's broke and grateful. If she refused, he sold her out and is living well on it. Either way he offers the hitman information on the borderland. Buy from him or not.
- **The exile (chapter 3, your friend's sleeve):** if the prince told the guard captain, his friend fled the capital and is on the road with the messenger. Help him through a checkpoint, or leave him to it. If the prince only asked him about it, he's still in the capital and the messenger mentions him instead.
- **The quartermaster's family (chapter 4, the hoard):** the hitman meets her family on the ground. If the hoard was kept secret, they're fed and wary. If it was exposed, they're starving. Share his supplies or not. If he does, they tell him her route up the tree, which means a faster ambush on Grounded.
