# Chapter 2: the story's structure

The shape of [Chapter 2, The Scavenger](chapter-2-design.md), agreed with the designer on 2026-10-07 ("let's go with this structure for now"). **(designer)** = decided in conversation; **(suggestion)** = Claude's proposal. Each part is planned in detail in `docs/sequences/chapter-2/` before it's built, the way Act 1 went day by day.

## Decided
- **Five parts, about eight playable days, with gaps between the parts** ("weeks later", "a month on") so hauls can build and suspicion can grow. **(designer: gaps are fine; break it into main parts)**
- **More combat than Act 1.** **(designer)** About eight to ten fights on the same system as Act 1.
- **The dog fights** as a party member. **(designer)**
- **Every borderland area carries story.** **(designer)**
- **The opening:** you scavenging, and trouble from **stragglers from elsewhere**, not the rival clan, whose first real appearance is Part 2's raid. **(designer)**
- **The first couple of days include bringing the dog home to your brother.** **(designer)**
- **The dog choice always ends with you keeping it.** Choosing to eat it: you're not a monster, and you keep it. **(designer)** Selling it is turned back too (design doc: your brother won't let it go, or it escapes and finds you again).
